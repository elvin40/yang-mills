**Claim (2), dependence chain: CONFIRMED.** In (2.12) every term outside the curly bracket is built from −(1/g_k²)A(U_k(V)), the Gaussian measure with C^{(k)} = (C*Δ^{(k)}C)^{−1}, and χ_k. None of these contains g_0, …, g_{k−1}. The p. 298 cancellation is stronger than the file says, and it is the right reading: Sect. 4 (OCR p. 291–292) treats the old counterterm fluctuation β_j(g_{j−1})(A(U_k(exp iB V)) − A(U_k(V))) (4.38) and shows its non-uniform parts (4.42), (4.44) "cancel" the marginal parts of the fluctuation of E^{(j)}, "because (4.38) appears with the minus sign in the effective action (1.6)". p. 298: "we should write the superscript (j) at the tensor". One small inaccuracy: (C2) says the log Z^{(j)} terms are "coupling-free". True, so they carry no g_i dependence; their irrelevance is not needed for the derivative.

**Claim (3), cube count and exponent: CONFIRMED.** In (0.30) Balaban sums Σ_{□∈π_j} O(1)(L^jη)^{4+α} = O(1)(L^jη)^α M^{−4}|T_1^{(k)}|, so a π_j cube has unit-lattice volume (M L^jη)^4. With η = L^{−k} ((1.2): "η = L^{−k}") a π_k cube holds L^{4(k−l)} π_l cubes. Per π_k cube: L^{4(k−l)} · L^{−(4+α)(k−l)} = L^{−α(k−l)}. The M^4 factors cancel. Correct.

**Claim (1), Lemma HH and the hop recursion: CORRECTED (minor).** The algebra is right. Put t_n = s_{i+1+n}. From t_{n+1} = θ Σ_{m≤n} L^{−α(n−m)} t_m one gets t_{n+1} = (L^{−α} + θ) t_n for n ≥ 1 and t_1 = θ t_0. So t_n = (θ/λ) λ^n S_0 exactly. The claimed bound s_l ≤ S_0 λ^{l−i−1} holds, and the induction sum ≤ λ/θ is right. The ∂β bound (C_Π/C_V) S_0 λ^{j−1−i} follows. Correction: the numerical check line "s_n = λ^n to rounding (ratio 1.0)" is false. Rerun of the same three cases gives s_60/λ^60 = 0.265, 0.393, 0.474 = θ/λ exactly; only the consecutive ratio s_{n+1}/s_n equals λ. The RATE is sharp; the constant is off by θ/λ (the bound is loose, not wrong).
"One hop per step, contraction θ per hop": this is the right structure IF (SM) holds, because each step re-feeds the whole past through the same bracket. The recursion double-feeds nothing: an old V^{(l)} enters step k+1 directly (weight L^{−α(k−l)}) and also through V^{(l+1)}, …, V^{(k)}; both routes are real, so summing them is correct, not double counting.

**θ = (big) · O(1)α_2^{−1}ε_1: plausible, UNSUPPORTED as a Lipschitz constant.** p. 280 says the t_□-derivative gives O(1)α_2^{−1}ε_1 and that this is how E_0 is "recovered". That is a bound for the SUM of all curly-bracket contributions to E^{(k+1)}, against an input of size E_0. It is consistent with a per-hop contraction, but Balaban does not separate per-l contributions, does not state the factor as Lipschitz, and the "big constant" of (3.54) is not given. Also the p. 280 factor is one of several; P^{(k)} also contributes to E^{(k+1)} and must fit in E_0. The file states all this in its open list. Grade of claim (5) below.

**Claim (4), slot definition: CONFIRMED, one caveat.** Check of double counting: by (2.15), 1/g_k² + Σ_{j<k} β_{j+1} = 1/g_0² on the trajectory, so the total coefficient of A(U_k) in (1.3) is the bare 1/g_0². The −β_{j+1}A pieces are bookkeeping inside V^{(j+1)}, not marginal inputs. So ∂β_j/∂g_i at fixed other slots, times v_i, is exactly the chain rule; the dg_k/dg_i dependence sits in v_k only. No double count. Caveat: the slot functions need Balaban's step-(k+1) bounds for histories OFF the trajectory (arbitrary h_k ∈ [0,γ]^{k+1}). Balaban's induction is stated along one trajectory with E^{(j)} a function of g_{j−1}; the extension is natural (the step integral uses only g_k and E_k) but is an extra assumption, close to hypothesis (I).
