\title{Conditional existence of four-dimensional Yang--Mills\\ on a finite torus along Ba{\l}aban's route}
\author{Claude}
\date{October 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Ba{\l}aban's renormalization-group construction of four-dimensional SU(2) lattice Yang--Mills theory depends on control of the running coupling. This control is his Theorem 2, whose proof was never published. We prove that a weak form of Theorem 2 follows from one uniform inequality (H2) on a two-step minimizer map, together with fixed-level inputs and a history condition. The weak form gives two-sided bounds on $1/g_k^2$ with an additive constant, and it suffices for Ba{\l}aban's Theorem 1. We prove the fixed-level inputs and the history condition relative to named inputs: Ba{\l}aban's published proofs, his inductive assumption (H0), stated conventions, and one external result on the constant-field effective action. At $L=13$, inside Ba{\l}aban's range $L>11$, we prove (H2) by a computer-assisted argument. An explicit majorant and an outward-rounded interval branch-and-bound give $\rho_1(j,K)^2\le10<13$ for every level $j$, including $j=\infty$, and every momentum $K$, so $\rho_2\le10<13$. The same argument gives (H2) at $L=15,17,21$. This proof uses a stated convention for Ba{\l}aban's averaging operator. The shooting lemmas, the arithmetic of Theorem K, and the algebraic core of the majorant are formalized in Lean 4. Second, on a fixed torus, we prove that a uniform moment bound (H-U) implies compactness of the lattice Schwinger functions of the centred plaquette density, and that every limit point satisfies the torus forms of the Osterwalder--Schrader axioms OS0--OS3. This is a compactness result; it is not an existence theorem for a continuum theory, and nontriviality of the limit is open. We reduce (H-U), but we do not prove it. It rests on sourced versions of Ba{\l}aban's bounds; on a hypothesis (FX), which we reduce to a holomorphy bound (H-hol) for Ba{\l}aban's increments, history bounds and three named inputs of the large-field step; and on a bridge from the source form to (H-U). A dependency table lists the hypotheses of each block. The arguments were produced and cross-checked by AI systems (Claude models); no human expert has reviewed them.
\end{abstract}
