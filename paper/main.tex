\title{Yang--Mills on a finite torus along Ba{\l}aban's route:\\ a verified reduction}
\author{Claude}
\date{October 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Ba{\l}aban's renormalization-group construction of four-dimensional SU(2) lattice Yang--Mills theory depends on control of the running coupling, his Theorem 2, whose proof was never published. We prove that a weak form of Theorem 2, namely two-sided bounds on $1/g_k^2$ with an additive constant, follows from one uniform inequality (H2) on a two-step minimizer map together with named fixed-level and history inputs; this weak form is enough for Ba{\l}aban's Theorem 1. At $L=13$, inside Ba{\l}aban's range $L>11$, the inequality (H2) is a certified theorem: an explicit majorant and an outward-rounded interval branch-and-bound give $\rho_1(j,K)^2\le10<13$ for every level $j$, including $j=\infty$, and every momentum $K$, so $\rho_2\le10<13$. This certificate uses our reading of Ba{\l}aban's averaging operator. The shooting lemmas, the arithmetic of Theorem K and the bridge lemmas of the certifier are formalized in Lean 4. Second, on a fixed torus, we prove that a uniform moment bound (H-U) gives compactness of the lattice Schwinger functions of the centred plaquette density, and that every limit point satisfies the torus forms of the Osterwalder--Schrader axioms OS0--OS3. The bound (H-U) is reduced, not proved. It rests on sourced versions of Ba{\l}aban's bounds; on a hypothesis (FX), which we reduce to a holomorphy bound (H-hol) for Ba{\l}aban's increments, history bounds and three named inputs of the large-field step; and on a bridge from the source form to (H-U). A dependency table records the status of each block.
\end{abstract}
