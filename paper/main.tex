\title{Yang--Mills on a finite torus along Ba{\l}aban's route:\\ a verified reduction}
\author{Claude}
\date{October 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Ba{\l}aban's renormalization-group construction of four-dimensional SU(2) lattice Yang--Mills theory rests on the control of the running coupling, his Theorem 2, whose proof was never published. We prove that a weak form of Theorem 2 (two-sided bounds on $1/g_k^2$ with an additive constant), which is enough for Ba{\l}aban's Theorem 1, follows from one uniform inequality (H2) on a two-step minimizer map together with named fixed-level inputs; the shooting lemmas of this step are also formalized in Lean 4. We prove that, on a fixed torus, a uniform moment bound (H-U) gives compactness of the lattice Schwinger functions of the centred plaquette density, and that every limit point satisfies the torus forms of the Osterwalder--Schrader axioms OS0--OS3. The bound (H-U) is reduced, not proved: it rests on sourced versions of Ba{\l}aban's bounds, on a hypothesis (FX) that is now reduced to one open strip bound, and on a bridge from the source form to (H-U), and one dependency table records the status of each block. Numerically, (H2) holds in floating point with margins of 31\% and 58\% at $L=3$ and $L=5$, and a rigorous interval enclosure holds at one point at $L=3$, but (H2) is certified at no $L$, no value exists in Ba{\l}aban's range $L>11$, and the result is not a solution of the Clay Millennium problem.
\end{abstract}
