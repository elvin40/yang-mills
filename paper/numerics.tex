Section~\ref{sec:cert} gives the plan for a certified (H2), whose open parts are listed again in Section~\ref{sec:remains}.

\subsection{One-step values}\label{sec:numonestep}

The arithmetic of Section~\ref{sec:onestep} shows that $\rho_1^2<L$ implies (H2). The measured one-step values are as follows.
\begin{itemize}
\item $L=3$: $\rho_1^2=3.10>3$. The one-step route fails (\S5.2); the two-step map is needed.
\item $L=5$: $\rho_1=1.860$ ($j=1$, Wilson fine kernel, dense, measured by the verifier), $1.9565$ ($j=2$), $1.963$ ($j=\infty$). So $\rho_1^2\le3.853<5$ and $\rho_1^2/L=0.771$ \status{numerical, CONFIRMED as a measurement, uncertified; \file{H2_LARGE_L_VERIFY} grade 4}.
\item $L=7$: $\rho_1\ge2.032$ (diagonal only), $\rho_1^2/L\approx0.59$ \status{numerical}.
\end{itemize}
So at $L\ge5$ the one-step map suffices for Theorem C1, as far as measured. For certification this replaces $L^8$ blocks per $K$ (two-step map) by $L^4$ blocks per $K$ (625$\times$ fewer at $L=5$).

\subsection{A rigorous point enclosure at $L=3$ \status{interval enclosure, verified}}\label{sec:enclosure}

Source: \file{H2_CERTIFY.md} with the prototype \file{numerics/h2cert_proto.py}; verifier \file{H2_CERTIFY_VERIFY.md}. At $L=3$, $j=\infty$, and the face point $K^*=(2.4035,2.4035,\pi,\pi)$ near the argmax of Section~\ref{sec:numH2}, ball arithmetic (python-flint) gives
\[
\rho_2(\infty,K^*)\in[1.9993,\,2.1273]<3 .
\]
The width comes from the umklapp tail and scales as $N^{-4}$ in the truncation $N$. The verifier reran the code at $N=12$ and obtained $[1.5475,\,2.5792]$, which contains the floating-point value $2.063361$ and confirms the $N^{-4}$ scaling; at $N=6$ the code stops with an assertion instead of returning a wrong interval. The verdict is CONFIRMED given the premises P1--P3 of \file{H2_CERTIFY_VERIFY}; P1 (the loop-family coefficients of the (0.4) row are exact rationals $k/648$, $k$ even) is proved there, and the fidelity of the loop family to Ba{\l}aban's paper stays inside P3. Further results of the same two files:
\begin{itemize}
\item One-step factorization: $\rho_2(j,K)\le\rho_1(j,K)\cdot\max_l\rho_1(j-1,k_l)$, exact \status{proved+verified}. So two-step work is needed only where the right side is $\ge L$, near the face diagonal.
\item Oishi--Rump bound $\|\mathfrak B^{-1}\|\in[42.7,\,43.6]$ at $K^*$ (bounds CONFIRMED). The values 177 and 195 of Section~\ref{sec:convergence} use the full $\Delta$ instead of $\tfrac14\Delta$; this factor reconciles them. No $\|\mathfrak B^{-1}\|$ enters $\rho_2$.
\item The umklapp tail enclosure is CONFIRMED for $M=\infty$. For finite $M$ it is CORRECTED: its lower bound must be 0, with class-wise factors $(\pi/2)^{12}$ for two or more large components.
\item A uniform Lipschitz grid is refuted as a route: $\Lambda_K\approx2.8\cdot10^4$ (CONFIRMED) would need about $10^{13}$ cells. The replacement is a per-cell invertibility test with a mean-value enclosure.
\item Cost: \file{H2_CERTIFY} estimates about $1.5\cdot10^4$ core-hours for a full certification at $L=3$. The verifier grades this estimate UNSUPPORTED: at the tested sizes the arb overhead dominates, and the box-sum speedup is overstated.
\end{itemize}
This is one point at one level. It does not certify the supremum over the Brillouin zone or over $j$.

\subsection{Certification plan and $j_0$ \status{designed, not done}}\label{sec:cert}
\file{B2_TWOSTEP_VERIFY} grade 6 (CORRECTED in size): certifying (H2) is a computer-assisted proof project, not a check. \file{H2_LARGE_L} \S4 gives the design; \file{H2_LARGE_L_VERIFY} grades 6--8 check it. Three parts.

(a) Tail $j\ge j_0$. From the resolvent identity, $\rho_2(j)\le R_\infty+(\rho_1^2/a_*)(\delta_{j-2}+\delta_{j-1})$, where $R_\infty=\rho_2$ at $j=\infty$, $a_*=\inf_Ka_j(K)$ with $\|[\mathfrak B^{-1}]_{f,f}\|=1/a_j(K)$ exactly, and $\delta_m=\sup_K\|\Delta_{L^m}-\Delta_\infty\|$ (O5 decay) [derivation CONFIRMED]. Inputs CORRECTED by the verifier: the 2-norm sup of $\delta_m$ is at the corner, $\delta=179,\,92.0,\,18.7,\,2.39,\,0.317$ ($M=1,3,9,27,81$), 45--90$\times$ the assumed value at small $m$; $a_*(L=5)=0.0107$, not 0.05. With these inputs, $j_0\approx8$ ($L=3$) and $\approx6$ ($L=5$). With the O6 coercivity constant, $j_0=25$, which cannot be used in practice (\file{B2_TWOSTEP_VERIFY} \S5 estimated $j\gtrsim45$ with $\|\mathfrak B^{-1}\|\le b(L)\sim10^{19}$).

(b) Levels $2\le j<j_0$ and $j=\infty$. A certified sup over the BZ of $\|H_{(2),j}(K)\|$ (at $L\ge5$: of $\|H_j(K)\|$, \S5.5). Plan: interval arithmetic with local mean-value enclosures on boxes in the sorted chamber (1/384 of the BZ), $4\times4$ and $5\times5$ verified inverses, one $4\times4$ $\lambda_{max}$ per $K$; about $2.5\cdot10^3$ boxes at half-width 0.05 at $L=3$; the analytic form of $\Delta$ near $K=0$, where $\rho_2(0)=1$. At $j=\infty$ also a certified tail bound for the Prop.\ A sum ($l_{max}$ truncation; observed effect $4\cdot10^{-4}$). There is no explicit global Lipschitz constant today: the A0/O5 strip width is existential, and the constant that can be made explicit is $\sim10^{40}$. Monotonicity in $j$ is not proved and is not used.

(c) Inputs to certify first: $\|\mathfrak B_j(K)^{-1}\|\le B_{num}$ for all $j$, $K$ (the O6 constant $b(L)$ is unusable), the O5 constant $C_0$ explicitly (or certified $\delta_m$), certified $a_*$, $\rho_1$, $R_\infty$.

The run plan (\file{H2_LARGE_L_VERIFY} grade 8) uses atomic JSON writes and pre-copied profile caches; the profile build time at $L\ge11$ is not budgeted. No certification run has been launched.
