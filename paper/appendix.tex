\section{Lean formalization}\label{app:lean}

The shooting lemmas of Section~\ref{sec:shooting}, the arithmetic of L-009 (Theorem K) and L-011, and the bridge lemmas of the certifier are formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib; seven files formalize lemmas of the sources block (Schur test, block-valued Schur test, Combes--Thomas, interpolation and rescaling, resolvent and weighted Neumann bounds, the gauge condition of the constrained minimizer, the slab counterexample to CMP 96 (2.127)). There are 80 Lean files, with no \texttt{sorry}. The axioms are propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound, except for the H2Box certificate: \texttt{H2Box.lean} and its 16 run files \texttt{H2BoxRun00--15} each add one \texttt{native\_decide} axiom, and the \texttt{H2Box.lean} theorems inherit them. The Lean support by ledger row is the table at the end of Section~\ref{sec:rem-ledger}, and \file{lean/README.md} lists every file. The list below gives the main formalized statements and the first 25 files.
