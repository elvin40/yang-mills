\paragraph{Item 7: the running-maximum lift.}
Item 7 asks for the own-slot bound of (G-gain) (H-111) on every past, also on the clamped, non-monotone pasts that the shooting evaluates.

\begin{convention}[reading of $\beta$ in item 7]\label{conv:src-beta-pure}
We read $\beta_{k+1}$ as the coefficient defined from the pure part of the step, as in Proposition~\ref{src:D3W8}. On the competing literal reading, $\beta_{k+1}$ is defined from the whole $E^{(k+1)}$, and the possible $g$-dependence of the newest R-input function $R^{(k)}$ is open.
\end{convention}

\begin{proposition}[item 7 on every past]\label{src:GGAIN-NONMONO}
Assume either (H0) (H-202) (ledger R-006), or the inputs of (G-gain) listed in ledger R-037. Assume Convention~\ref{conv:src-beta-pure} and the side condition $4\gamma^{\kappa_0}\le E_0$. Then for every step $k$, every past $h\in\ioc{\gamma}^k$, monotone or not, and every $g\in[0,\gamma]$,
\[
|\beta_{k+1}(h,g)-\beta_{k+1}(0)|\le C_\beta g,\qquad C_\beta=C_\Pi S_0,
\]
where $C_\Pi S_0$ is the constant of (G-gain). The bound is uniform in $k$, $K$, and the past.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof (sketch)]
The proof of (G-gain) uses monotonicity at one place only: Step 2 of L-164, where $\max_{j\le k}4g_j^{\kappa_0}=4g_k^{\kappa_0}$ compares bound sizes. Put $G_k=\max_{i\le k}g_i$, the running maximum. The induction $a_j\le2G_j^{\kappa_0}$ then holds for every past under $4\gamma^{\kappa_0}\le E_0$, a condition of Ba{\l}aban's type. The constant does not change, because $E_0+4\gamma^{\kappa_0}\le2E_0$ is an input radius that (G-gain) already covers. The value at zero own coupling, (K1), is free of the history for any input, because the exponent vanishes at $g_k=0$ (CMP 109 p.~268). The endpoint $g\to0$ is a dominated-convergence step, which we do not write out line by line.
\end{proof}

\emph{Hypotheses.} Under (H0) (ledger R-006), the use here reduces to the residue N1--N9 of Section~\ref{sec:src-r5}. Under (G-gain), the inputs of ledger R-037 are (Q)/$c_-$, ($M_r$), CMP 102 (189), and the (M-B) (H-145) bookkeeping. The result also uses L5, the evaluation of $\beta$ off Ba{\l}aban's trajectory (item 8, ledger R-031). We assume, in addition, that Ba{\l}aban's R-operation bounds CMP 119 (2.31) and the off-trajectory small-field induction of \file{SOURCES_SM.md} \S5.1 contain no further use of the order of the couplings.

\begin{proposition}[the window on every admissible history]\label{src:T2-KUNIF}
By CMP 119 (2.24), p.~253, and p.~279, $\beta_j$ is the whole-lattice coefficient of CMP 109, whose $\Pi$ in (4.37) is a sum over $\mathbb Z^4$. So the $\beta_j$ of the recursion does not depend on $K$. Assume:
(K1) $\beta_j(h,0)=\beta_j(0)$ for every admissible past;
(K2) $|\partial\beta_j/\partial g_{j-1}|\le C_\beta$;
(K3) the window bound $\sum_{i=k+1}^{n}\beta_i(0)\in[b(n-k)-2R,\,b(n-k)+2R]$ of Theorem K (L-009).
If $\gamma\le b/(4C_\beta)$, then $T(m,n)\in[(3b/4)(n-m)-2R,\,(5b/4)(n-m)+2R]$ on every admissible history.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof (sketch)]
Lemmas DS and DT run at one fixed $K$. They never compare two trajectories or two values of $K$. So only their constants must be free of $K$. The bound $|\beta_{k+1}(h_k)-\beta_{k+1}(0)|\le C_\beta g_k$ follows from (K1) and the mean-value theorem in the own slot. It gives the window bracket pointwise on each clamped history of the intermediate-value argument.
\end{proof}

\emph{Hypotheses.} (G-gain) alone gives $C_\beta$ only on monotone slot histories. So (K2) needs item 7, which Proposition~\ref{src:GGAIN-NONMONO} gives from (H0) (ledger R-006) or from (G-gain) (ledger R-037). (K3) rests on the inputs of Theorem K. \paragraph{Item 6: (G2) (ledger R-030).}

\begin{proposition}[(G2) at fixed level]\label{src:T2-G2}
Fix the level $j$. Assume that $\tilde Q$ is surjective and that the averaging is analytic in the background. Then:
(i) the constrained one-loop log-determinant on a torus of side $N$ has no zero modes;
(ii) its density per coarse cell converges to the infinite-lattice density with error $\le C_je^{-\delta_jN}$, also for every derivative in the plane-wave amplitude;
(iii) the $p^2$-coefficient commutes with $N\to\infty$;
(iv) if in addition the gap input (Gap$_f$) holds and the density is $C^4$ in $f$, then the $f^2$-coefficient at the constant field commutes with $N\to\infty$.

\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof (sketch)]
The proof uses a Combes--Thomas decay bound and the method of images. The constants are fixed-$j$ constants, with $\delta_j\gtrsim L^{-2j}$.
\end{proof}

We do not prove a version that is uniform in $j$.

\paragraph{One sourced step at one loop (D1).}

\begin{proposition}[the sourced step at one loop]\label{src:D1}
Let the plaquette-density source enter as the local bare coupling $w_p=1/g_0^2-f_p$. Then at one loop, for every background:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] the multiplicative source renormalization is $c_1=1$ exactly;
\item[(b)] the first correction is $c_1-1=\tfrac12g^3\beta_1'(g)$; it is $\beta_1^{(2)}g_0^4+O(g_0^6)$ if $\beta_1$ is even, and $O(g_0^3)$ under a $C^1$ bound only;
\item[(c)] the field-independent terms are the tadpole $\tfrac12(9L^4-9)g_0^2$ per coarse cell ($\tfrac34(1-L^{-4})g_0^2$ per plaquette) and a contact term $C(q)$ that is analytic at $q=0^+$;
\item[(d)] the first field-dependent gradient terms have dimension 6, by index parity.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof (sketch)]
The source enters the step only through the Hessian $\Delta_f=\sum_pw_pH_p$ and a current term $\langle J_f,B\rangle$ at Ba{\l}aban's unweighted minimizer. Gauge fixing, ghosts, and the constraint carry no $f$. For constant $f$ the quadratic form scales by $1-g_0^2f_0$. So the determinant changes by a field-independent constant over $9L^4-9$ modes per cell. The error of the expansion point has the small parameter $Lp$.
\end{proof}

Per-momentum formulas for the tadpole, $C(q)$, and the mixed derivative are evaluated by \file{numerics/d1_check.py}. They agree with real-space finite differences on the periodic $6^4$ lattice. Numerically, $|c_1-1|\le5\times10^{-4}$. The data do not show geometric convergence in the grid, and we do not use the numerical values of $\gamma_2$. The non-Gaussian sourced step and the pointwise flow are the subject of Propositions~\ref{src:D2}--\ref{src:D3W8} and of (B-S) (ledger R-035).

\begin{theorem}[the lattice log limit (L-LL), H-137]\label{thm:L040}
Let $W(\varphi;m^2)$ be the per-cell charged bracket $-\frac12\log|\det|(A_\varphi+m^2)+\log|\det|(\mathcal G_\varphi+m^2)$ minus its value at $\varphi=0$, for the constant abelian background of plaquette angle $\varphi$, and $H_\varphi(\mu)=\varphi^{-2}[W(\varphi;0)-W(\varphi;\mu|\varphi|)]$. \]

\begin{proof}[Proof (sketch)]
