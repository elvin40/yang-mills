## 1. What GM prove (summary, for reference)

- Model: 3d U(1) lattice gauge theory with Villain action, in a heat bath of noncompact electrodynamics outside a finite Λ (App. A, eq. A.2). Studied through its exact dual, the Z-ferromagnet on (aZ)³: Z_Λ = Σ_{n∈(2πZ)^Λ} exp L(n), L(n) = −(1/(2β)) ∫_x [∇_μ n(x)]² (eqs. 1.1–1.2), boundary condition: massless free field outside Λ.
- Mass: a²m_D² = (2β/a) e^{−β v_Cb(0)/2} (eq. 1.8a); v_Cb(0) = 0.2527 a^{−1} (eq. 1.8b). Abstract: m_D² = (2β/a³) e^{−β v_Cb(0)/2}.
- Theorem 1 (p. 3): string tension α ≥ c m_D β^{−1}, some c > 0, for β/a sufficiently large (eq. 1.9). Threshold and c are not explicit. GM state a classical approximation α_class = 8 m_D β^{−1} and say the estimates for the true asymptotics "are lacking".
- Corollary 2 (pp. 3–4): α > 0 for all g² > 0. Proof: Theorem 1 plus monotonicity of αa² in β/a by the 3d Guth inequality (lemma 4.1 of their ref. 18).
- Theorem 10 (§5): strong-coupling (small β/a) lower bound on α by a Peierls expansion on the original lattice.
- Proposition 3 (pp. 5–6, eqs. 1.16–1.18): after integrating out high frequencies with Pauli–Villars cutoff M = λ^{−1}m_D, the effective action is a sine-Gordon action with bounded corrections, for λ small and β/a large (depending on λ).
- Method: Yukawa gas representation (§2), iterated Mayer expansions (§3–4, from their ref. 6), Glimm–Jaffe–Spencer expansion adapted from Brydges–Federbush (§6–7: Peierls expansion of domain walls on a block lattice, translation of φ, cluster expansion), area law (§8, Lemma 17), continuum limit (§9).
- Theorem 4 (pp. 9–10): with M = (β/a)^{1/12}m_D (eq. 9.2) and field ψ of eq. 1.24, correlations at fixed distances in units m_D^{−1} converge, as β/a → ∞, to those of the Euclidean free scalar field of mass m_D. String tension in units m_D² tends to ∞.
- Mass gap (end of §7, p. 60): by "doubling the measure" and Lemma 15, truncated Wilson-loop correlations decay exponentially; the glue-ball mass "is asymptotically bounded from below by m_D. The details are left to the reader." The matching upper bound: "the estimates to prove it are lacking."
- Remark F (pp. 17–18): uniqueness of the equilibrium state is NOT proved: "A proof of unicity would be desirable."

## 3. Regime and type of result, in one place

- Action: Villain only (abstract; App. A). Wilson action: not treated.
- Dimension and model: pure 3d U(1) on (aZ)³, no matter fields, heat-bath (noncompact) boundary condition outside Λ.
- Coupling: β_GM/a = 4π²/(g²a) "sufficiently large" for Theorem 1, Proposition 3, Lemmas 15–17; no explicit threshold. Small β/a: Theorem 10 (§5). All β: Corollary 2 (α > 0 only, no rate). GM call β/a a temperature for the Z-ferromagnet and an inverse temperature for the gauge theory (p. 1). No physical temperature or compact time direction appears.
- Lattice spacing: Theorem 1 is at fixed a (the bound scales with a by dimension). Theorem 4 is a continuum limit a → 0 at fixed m_D.
- Type of result: Theorem 1 is a Wilson-loop area law (string tension lower bound). Theorem 4 is a continuum limit to a free massive field. The lattice mass gap at fixed large β/a is sketched only (end of §7), as a lower bound ≥ m_D asymptotically, for Wilson-loop correlations; the dual-field version follows from Lemma 16 plus the same argument but is not written. So the answer to "gap, area law, or both" is: area law proved; gap sketched (lower bound only); continuum free-field limit proved.

## 4. What GM do NOT cover that R3S1_GAP needs

1. Root-lattice (U(1)^{N−1}) charges and N species with independent activities. GM: one Z species.
2. Fugacity untied from β (z ≤ e^{−cβ} with c set by C_−/κ_3). GM: z = e^{−β v(0)/2} exactly; the tie enters the cosine period and the covariance as well.
3. Explicit constants. GM print no c, no β_0, no c_0. Item G4 has nothing to compare against.
4. An extensive family of local insertions Π(1 + K_X) (G2). GM: finitely many periodic observables (Lemma 16) and one Wilson loop (Lemma 15).
5. An extra Gaussian field (holonomy φ) coupled linearly to the charges (Yukawa pair term, §4.2(b′)).
6. Non-Villain (Wilson-type or block-effective) weights for the Cartan angle, beyond GM's own Proposition 3 corrections.
7. Uniqueness of the infinite-volume Gibbs state (Remark F says it is open).
8. A printed fixed-β mass-gap theorem with rate m_D(1+o(1)). GM have a sketched lower bound and an unproved belief for the upper bound.
9. Any boundary condition other than the noncompact heat bath; in particular periodic or slab-induced boundary conditions from R³ × S¹ at finite ℓ.
10. 4d origin: GM say nothing about dimensional reduction, Polyakov loops, center symmetry, or SU(N). (Expected; listed for completeness.)

## 5. Net effect on R3S1_GAP

- The numerical and formula content of §4.1 (dualities, v(0), z, m_D² = 8π²βz, √z scaling of the gap) is CONFIRMED.
- The description of GM's method in (GM-2) is wrong, and the "complex translation" in (H5) does not exist. §4.2(b) and §5 risk 2 must be re-aimed at Proposition 3 and the GJS steps (6.1)–(6.13).
- [C4]/G4 is not a check against printed constants. It folds into G3 (redo Prop. 3 and §6–7 with untied fugacity, track constants).
- [C5]/G5: the gap lower bound is derivable from Lemma 16 with a short argument that GM leave to the reader; uniqueness is open in GM. G5 should be split: G5a gap lower bound (short, from Lemma 16), G5b uniqueness (new).
- The sharp target "m = (c/L)(g²N)^{−3/2}e^{−4π²/(g²N)}(1+o(1))" lacks GM support for the (1+o(1)) even in U(1). Only the lower bound is in reach from GM's methods.
