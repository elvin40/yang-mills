## V2. CMP 116 pp. 12–16 [V images 130 dpi, p. 13 zoom 300 dpi]
- p. 13 verbatim: "We define σ_0 as the family of such cubes Δ disjoint with the interior of Z̃_0, or with the interior of Z̃′_0 ... SOURCES 1.1 CONFIRMED (cube size LM; "Z̃_0" with tilde in the σ_0 clause; "Z_0" without tilde in the dist clause).
- (2.5) [V]: C^{(k)}(Z_0) is the covariance of exp(−½⟨Z_0B, C*Δ_kCZ_0B⟩) on Z_0, i.e. C_1 = (Z_0AZ_0)^{−1}. CONFIRMED.
- (2.8) [V]: "Σ_Z Π_{Δ⊂Z\Z̃′_0} ∫_0^1 ds(Δ) ∂/∂s(Δ) ∫dμ_0(X)G(Z_0, X, C^{(k)}(Z_0, s(Z)), (C^{(k)})^{1/2}(s(Z)), Δ_k(s(Z)))". ONE variable s(Δ) PER CUBE, and the evaluation point s(Z) has s(Δ) ∈ [0,1] inside Z, 0 outside. So all real points needed lie in [0,1]^{σ_0}. The corner reduction (SOURCES 3.2) applies to exactly this parametrization.
- (2.14) [V]: the s-derivatives are written as Cauchy integrals "∫ dσ(Δ)/(2πi(σ(Δ) − s(Δ))²)". The integrand contains "∫dμ_{C^{(k)}(Z_0,σ(Z))}(B)" at COMPLEX σ. p. 15: "We consider it as an analytic function of (U, J) ... and of the complex parameters σ(Z), τ. This complicates estimates ... because the operators in it are not symmetric, and the second measure is complex. ... For the pair (U, 0) the operators are symmetric, and the measure is positive, and then the estimates are simpler. The general case is handled by a perturbative argument."
- (2.15) [V]: Balaban bounds the complex measure by |det(C^{(k)}(Z_0,σ(Z))^{−1})/det(Re C^{(k)}(Z_0,σ(Z))^{−1})|^{1/2} ∫dμ_{(Re C^{(k)}(Z_0,σ(Z))^{−1})^{−1}}. This needs Re C(σ)^{−1} > 0 at complex σ on the Cauchy circles.
- p. 15–16 [V]: "we replace the operators by the corresponding operators with σ(Z) = 0, U = U, J = 0, and we estimate the error"; (2.16) |R_1(b,b′)| ≤ (O(1)e^{−⅓δ_0M} + O(α_0 + α_1)) exp(−½δ_0|b_− − b′_−|); "M is much bigger than κ_1, especially that e^{−⅓δ_0M}e^{16κ_1} < 1".
- Consequence 1: Balaban's comparison point is σ(Z) = 0, not s = 1; R_1 in (2.16) is the error in the first-exponential form ⟨Γ_kX, C^{(k)}(Z_0,σ)Γ_kX⟩, not C_s^{−1} − C_1^{−1}. SOURCES 2.3 "reproduces (2.16)" is suggestive only [I], as the author marks.
- Consequence 2: e^{16κ_1} next to e^{−⅓δ_0M} indicates that Balaban pays the complex-σ growth with the M-factor, not with the M_1^{−1/2} walk factor as SOURCES 5.3 suggests. SOURCES 5.3 mechanism is [I] and possibly wrong; its scope note stands.
- Zero-length walk: σ_0 cubes are disjoint from int Z̃_0 = int X̃_0^5, so they meet X̃_0^5 at most on the boundary. If boundary contact counted as "intersect", C(Z_0, 0) would lose the leading term R_0(X_0) and could not be O(e^{−⅓δ_0M})-close to anything useful; (2.16) and its use in (2.15) force the "shares interior" reading. SOURCES 2.3 [I] SUPPORTED by this consistency argument.
