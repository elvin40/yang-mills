## 1. What a "walk expansion with (3.108) bounds" is (definition used below)
1.1 Printed templates [V, text l. 1209–1226 + 1331–1347; image p. 416 checked in SOURCES_A2_R2_VERIFY V2 and SOURCES_A3_SIGMA_VERIFY V4]. Thm 3.9 (3.98): (Q′G′²Q′*)^{−1} = Σ_ω R′_0(X_0)R′_{α1}(X_1)···R′_{αn}(X_n), walks with X_{i−1} ∩ X_i ≠ ∅. Thm 3.10 (3.107): the same for G. The factors R′_α(X) are defined on p. 413 [V text]: "it is localized in X, i.e. its kernel has a support in X × X, it depends on U restricted to X^5, and satisfies a bound of the type (3.89)". R′_0(□) = h_□C_□h_□. Domains X = small connected unions of cubes of 𝒟 (largest = □_0^{(2)}, a union of 5^d cubes).
1.2 Definition (W-form) [P, a precise version of 1.1]. Fix the multiscale block lattice 𝔅 (A1_KERNEL §5), the metric d, the measure μ, the weight m(y,y′) = (μ(y)μ(y′))^{−1/2}. A walk expansion of an operator T on ℓ²(𝔅,μ) with constants (B, ρ, δ) is a family {(X, α, R_α(X))} and a representation
 T = Σ_ω T_ω, T_ω := R_0(X_0)R_{α1}(X_1)···R_{αn}(X_n), ω = ((0,X_0),…,(α_n,X_n)), X_{i−1} ∩ X_i ≠ ∅,
 such that:
 (L1) supp kernel R_α(X) ⊂ X × X; (L2) R_α(X) depends on U only through U|_{X^5} (and on domain data only in X^5);
 (B1) |T_ω(y,y′)| ≤ B·ρ^{|ω|}·m(y,y′)·e^{−δ d(ω,y,y′)}, with d(ω,y,y′) the path length (3.93) through all domains;
 (B2) #{(α, X): X ∩ X′ ≠ ∅} ≤ C_𝒟 for each X′ (bounded branching), and C_𝒟ρ ≤ ¼.
 Then Σ_ω |T_ω(y,y′)| ≤ 2B·m(y,y′)e^{−δ d(y,y′)} (absolute convergence; d(ω,y,y′) ≥ d(y,y′) by the triangle inequality).
1.3 Match with (3.108) [P]. Balaban's factor O(1)O(M^{−1/2})^{|ω|}·M^{−½|ω|} = O(1)·(cM^{−1})^{|ω|}: one half controls the count (B2), the other half is the free small factor M^{−½|ω|} that the s-rule and the remainder bounds use (CMP 116 p. 3: "The first two factors … control the sum over ω"). So (B1)–(B2) with ρ = c/M is the (3.108) form, up to the rate δ and the scale powers (L^jη)^{±}, which m and the rescaling S of A1_KERNEL §8 carry.
1.4 Why the s-rule needs (L1)–(L2), not only kernel decay [P]. The s-rule (CMP 116 p. 3 [V via A3_SIGMA_VERIFY V1]) multiplies T_ω by Π s(Δ) over σ_0 cubes that meet X̃_0^5 ∪ … ∪ X̃_n^5. For s(Δ) = 0 the surviving terms must not see Δ: neither in their kernel support (L1) nor in their U-dependence (L2). A global inverse from Lemma CT (A1_KERNEL §7) has neither property: its kernel and its U-dependence are global. This is the gap that SOURCES_A2_R2_VERIFY V8 records.
1.5 Norm used throughout [P]. For an operator A on ℓ²(𝔅,μ) and κ ≥ 0 put ‖A‖_κ := sup_f ‖e^{κf}Ae^{−κf}‖, sup over all f: 𝔅 → ℝ with |f(y) − f(y′)| ≤ d(y,y′). Properties: (N1) ‖AB‖_κ ≤ ‖A‖_κ‖B‖_κ (conjugation is multiplicative); (N2) |a(y,y′)| ≤ ‖A‖_κ m(y,y′)e^{−κd(y,y′)} (take f = d(·,y′), as in the Lemma CT proof); (N3) if |a(y,y′)| ≤ c m(y,y′)e^{−κ′d(y,y′)} with κ′ > κ, then ‖A‖_κ ≤ c·Σ(κ′ − κ) (Schur test, A1_KERNEL §5, §7). (N3′) if in addition |a| carries a factor d(y,y′): ‖A‖_κ ≤ c·Σ′(κ′ − κ), Σ′ the Schur constant with weight (1 + d). Lemma CT's proof shows exactly ‖N̂^{−1}‖_ε ≤ 2/c_−.

 So local positivity comes from the GLOBAL (C) by restriction, plus a truncation error. It is not A3.
4.3 Local inverse with uniform decay [P, Lemma CT, accretive form]. Lemma CT (A1_KERNEL §7) holds verbatim for a non-symmetric N̂_□ with Re⟨u,N̂_□u⟩ ≥ c‖u‖²: the proof uses only Re⟨u, N̂_εu⟩ ≥ (c − ‖E‖)‖u‖², which gives ‖N̂_εu‖ ≥ (c − ‖E‖)‖u‖, hence invertibility in finite dimension and ‖N̂_ε^{−1}‖ ≤ 1/(c − ‖E‖). ## 5. Parametrix, remainder, walk series [P]
5.1 Parametrix identity. P := Σ_□ h_□C_□h_□. Since supp h_□ ⊂ □, N̂_□C_□ = 1_□ and h_□1_□ = h_□:
 N̂h_□C_□h_□ = (1 − 1_□)N̂h_□C_□h_□ + 1_□(N̂ − N̂^{(□)})1_□h_□C_□h_□ + N̂_□h_□C_□h_□,
 N̂_□h_□C_□h_□ = h_□N̂_□C_□h_□ + [N̂_□, h_□]C_□h_□ = h_□² + [N̂_□, h_□]C_□h_□.
 Summing with Σh_□² = 1: N̂P = I + Σ_□ F_□, F_□ := (E^sep_□ + E^tr_□ + E^com_□)C_□h_□, with
 E^sep_□ := (1 − 1_□)N̂h_□ (separation), E^tr_□ := 1_□(N̂ − N̂^{(□)})1_□h_□ (truncation; Balaban's domain change), E^com_□ := [N̂_□, h_□] (commutator).
 This is CMP 96 (2.82) / CMP 99 (3.95) term by term [V p. 237 image], with G′(□) → G^{(□)}.
5.2 Bounds per cube, in ‖·‖_κ, κ := ε (§1.5; ‖·‖_κ is monotone in κ, so ‖C_□‖_κ ≤ 4/c_−; ‖h_□‖_κ ≤ 1).
 (sep) kernel of E^sep_□ is n̂(y,z)h_□(z) with y ∉ □, z ∈ □°, so d(y,z) ≥ M_1. e^{−δ_Gd} ≤ e^{−½(δ_G−κ)M_1}e^{−κd}e^{−½(δ_G−κ)d}. By (N3): ‖E^sep_□‖_κ ≤ c_+Σ(½(δ_G − κ))e^{−½(δ_G−κ)M_1}. Since κ ≤ ½δ_G: ≤ c_+Σ(¼δ_G)e^{−¼δ_GM_1}.
 (tr) by 4.2(iii) and (N3) with κ ≤ ½δ_G... the margin is zero at κ = ½δ_G, so use 4.2(iii) with the split ¾/¼: |kernel| ≤ 2c_+e^{−δ_GM_1}m e^{−¾δ_Gd}; ‖E^tr_□‖_κ ≤ 2c_+Σ(¼δ_G)e^{−δ_GM_1}.
 (com) kernel n̂_□(y,z)(h_□(z) − h_□(y)), |·| ≤ 2c_+(c_h/M_1)m·d(y,z)e^{−δ_Gd}; (N3′): ‖E^com_□‖_κ ≤ 2c_+c_hΣ′(¼δ_G)/M_1.
 Hence, with (N1): ‖F_□‖_κ ≤ ρ_1 := (4/c_−)·2c_+[Σ(¼δ_G)(e^{−¼δ_GM_1} + e^{−δ_GM_1}) + c_hΣ′(¼δ_G)M_1^{−1}] ≤ K_F/M_1, K_F = K_F(d, L, a, a_0) (M_1 ≥ 4/δ_G·log M_1 absorbs the exponentials).
5.3 Localization of the error terms (to get (L1)–(L2)). E^com_□C_□h_□ and E^tr-free parts: E^com_□ is supported in □ × □ and depends on U only on □⁺: it is one factor with domain □⁺. E^sep_□ and E^tr_□ contain the global N̂: expand N̂ = Σ_{ω′}N̂_{ω′} by (W_G) and keep each G-walk ω′ as a chain of factors (its own domains X′_i, U on X̃′_i^5). This is Balaban's step on CMP 99 p. 411 ("replace the propagator G by the expansion (3.90)"). Each G-walk factor carries c_G/M_1 from (3.108); the separation (resp. truncation) gives the extra e^{−¼δ_GM_1} (resp. e^{−δ_GM_1}) once per E-factor, taken from the path length d(ω′,y,z) ≥ M_1 (resp. ≥ 4M_1). The factor C_□h_□ (no small factor) is attached to the E-factor on its left, as on p. 412 ("We attach each operator … to a closest operator … on the right").
5.4 The walk series. N̂^{−1} = P(I + ΣF_□)^{−1} = Σ_{n≥0} P(−ΣF_□)^n, once convergence is shown (5.5). Expanding P and each F_□ (5.3), every term is a product
 T_ω = Φ_0Φ_1···Φ_n, Φ_0 = h_{□_0}C_{□_0}h_{□_0} (domain X_0 = □_0⁺), Φ_i ∈ {G-walk factors, E^com C h, the last factor of an E^sep/E^tr chain with C_□h_□ attached},
 each Φ_i localized in X_i × X_i (X_i a small connected union of 𝒟-cubes: □⁺ or a (W_G) domain) and depending on U only on X̃_i^5 (or □⁺); consecutive domains intersect, because the left variable of Φ_{i+1} lies in the support of the right side of Φ_i. So T_ω has (L1)–(L2) [P, given the locality of (W_G) factors (Q, Q* block-local [I])].
5.5 Bound per walk. Insert the kernels and sum over intermediate points y_1, …, y_n (y_i ∈ X_{i−1} ∩ X_i). With (N2) per factor, |φ_i(y_i,y_{i+1})| ≤ ‖Φ_i‖_κ m(y_i,y_{i+1})e^{−κd(y_i,y_{i+1})}. The weights m telescope: μ(y_i)m(y_{i−1},y_i)m(y_i,y_{i+1}) = m(y_{i−1},y_{i+1}). Split κ = ½κ + ½κ: ½κ builds e^{−½κ d(ω,y,y′)} (path through all domains, (3.93)); the other ½κ pays the sum over each y_i: Σ_{y_i} e^{−½κ d(y_{i−1},y_i)} ≤ c_1(½κ) [R CMP 96 Lemma 2.1 (2.61)]. Result:

## 7. Conclusion and consumers
7.1 Theorem A1-W [P given the inputs listed in §8]. For regular U ((3.35)–(3.36)), Mα_0 ≤ a_0, M_1 ≥ M_W(d, L, a, a_0), the operator (QGQ*)^{−1} has a walk expansion Σ_ωT_ω in the sense of §1.2: factors localized in X × X (X = small connected unions of M_1-cubes: □⁺ or Balaban's G-domains), U-dependence on X̃_0^5 ∪ … ∪ X̃_n^5, and
 |T_ω(y,y′)| ≤ (4/c_−)L^{d/2+1}(L^jη)^{−1−d/2}(L^{j′}η)^{−1−d/2}·(K_W/M_1)^{|ω|}·e^{−½ε d(ω,y,y′)},

7.2 Where uniformity in U enters [P]: only through (i) c_− of (C) (bump extension, uniform in regular U) and (ii) the constants of (W_G) (Balaban, uniform in regular U).
