1.3 Lemma D (dressing) [P]. For every family F:
 (a) Q_F(Dλ) = D^{(1)}(λ|_centres);
 (b) Q_str(Dλ) = D^{(1)}λ̄, with λ̄(y) := Σ_{x∈B(y)}L^{−d}λ(x);
 (c) Q_FA = Q_strA − D^{(1)}r_F(A);
 (d) the linear part at U = 1 of the one-step dressed average (89) (equivalently (3.113)) is Q^{dr}_FA := −r_F(A)(c−) + (Q_FA)(c) + r_F(A)(c+) = (Q_strA)(c).
Proof. (a) For each x and each pair of contours the chain telescopes: (λ(x) − λ(c−)) + (λ(x′) − λ(x)) − (λ(x′) − λ(c+)) = λ(c+) − λ(c−). The means over F and over x keep this value. (b) x ↦ x′ is a bijection B(c−) → B(c+), so Σ_xL^{−d}(λ(x′) − λ(x)) = λ̄(c+) − λ̄(c−). (c) Expand Q_F: the first chain term gives r_F(A)(c−); the last gives −Σ_{x∈B(c−)}L^{−d}⟨A(Γ_{c+,x′})⟩ = −r_F(A)(c+), by the same bijection; the middle term is Q_strA. (d) In (89) at V_0 = 1 we have R_{0,c} = 1 and V̄_0 = 1. The linear part of (R̄_{0,c−}V_1)^{−1} is −r_F(A)(c−), that of (V_1V_0)‾_c is (Q_FA)(c), and that of R̄_{0,c+}V_1 is r_F(A)(c+) (§1.2). Sum and use (c): Q_strA − D^{(1)}r_F(A) + D^{(1)}r_F(A) = Q_strA. ∎
Page check [V]. On gradients (d) gives Q^{dr}_F(Dλ) = D^{(1)}λ̄ by (b). This is the identity that Balaban derives on CMP 99 p. 418, Q_jDλ = D̄^jQ′_jλ with Q′ the block average at U = 1. The undressed Q_F gives D^{(1)}(λ|_centres) instead, by (a). So the page confirms that CMP 99's Q(1) is the dressed one and not the undressed Q_F.
Numerical check [N] (numerics/l1a_dressing.py; d = 2, L = 3, a 9 × 9 periodic lattice, random A and λ; the axial family and the family of all monotone shortest paths). Maximum errors: |Q_F − (Q_str − Dr_F)| ≤ 4.5·10^{−16}; |Q^{dr}_F − Q_str| ≤ 4.5·10^{−16}; |Q_F(Dλ) − D(λ|_centres)| ≤ 4.5·10^{−16}; |Q^{dr}_F(Dλ) − Dλ̄| ≤ 6.2·10^{−16}. Both families.
Remark [P]. The nonabelian linear part is the same computation: (0.8) makes the linear part of every M the arithmetic mean, and the parallel transports R(V_0(·)) are 1 at V_0 = 1. So Lemma D holds for every M with (A), (0.5)–(0.9).
