- Step 7: d/du[4p_R n log ℓ_2(u)] = 4p_R n C_β/(u ln L · (1+C_β log_L u) · ℓ_2(u)) ≤ 4p_R C_β/(ln L · ℓ_2(n)) for u ≥ n. Author drops the factor (1+C_β log_L u) ≥ 1, still an upper bound. ℓ_2(2) can be small (L=13, C_β=1: ≈0.24), so κ_1 is large but fixed. Correct.

- Caveat (new): (FX2) gives each polymer the radius of its COARSEST scale m. A multi-scale polymer that contains fine-scale R/B/e terms (scale m' < m) has a radius no larger than τ_{m'} ∝ R̄_{m'}^{-4} < τ_m. Step 3 and Step 7 depend on this choice. This is a strength assumption inside (FX), not an error in the arithmetic.
