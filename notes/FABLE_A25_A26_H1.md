1.2 Dressing Lemma [P]. The one-step fluctuation average of CMP 98 §C / CMP 99 (3.113) is: bring U′ to the chain-axial gauge "U′(y, x) = 1 for all x ∈ B(y)" ((64) with the chain variable of the family), normalize the gauge transformation by (81) (its gauge average is 1), and take the undressed average Q_G of the result. Its linear part at U = 1 is
 Q^{dr}_GA = Q_G(A − Dλ), λ(x) := ⟨A(Γ_{y(x),x})⟩ + λ(y(x)), with λ̄ = 0, i.e. λ(y) = −r_G(A)(y).
 By 1.1(a), Q_G(Dλ) = D^{(1)}(λ|_{centres}) = −D^{(1)}r_G(A). By 1.1(c), Q_GA = Q_strA − D^{(1)}r_G(A). Hence
 Q^{dr}_GA = Q_strA − D^{(1)}r_G(A) + D^{(1)}r_G(A) = Q_strA. ∎
 Cross-check on the page [V, CMP 99 p. 418]: for U′ = u·1·u^{−1} (A = −Dλ) Balaban gets Ū′_c = (Ru)(c_−)R_c(Ru)^{−1}(c_+), linear part −D^{(1)}λ̄; (3.114) reads QDλ = D^{(1)}Q′λ with Q′ = block average. That is 1.1(b) for Q_str, not 1.1(a) for Q_G. So CMP 99's one-step Q at U = 1 is Q_str, chain-free, for the axial family; the same computation with the symmetric family and any group average M with (0.8) (linear part = arithmetic mean) gives the same Q_str. The chain terms of the loop average cancel exactly against the gauge normalization (81)/(3.113), for every family and every M.
 Direct consequence. (3.114)–(3.115), QDλ = D_ŪQ′λ with Q′ = block average, hold for CMP 109's averages at U = 1 verbatim [P]; at regular U they are the covariance identity of p. 418, which uses only (0.6) (SOURCES_P253_P275_VERIFY U7).
