## 2. The three thresholds (file §1.3–1.4)

C1. (E) extension, g-free. The p. 193 extension has \|∂V − 1\| < O(1)M²ε on Z [V]. U_{k,Z} is defined for \|∂V_k − 1\| < a_1 on Z [V p. 194, (1.77)]. So the threshold is O(1)M²ε < a_1 (or the file's ½ε_1^{102}, which is smaller). No g. CONFIRMED [V].
C2. (C) contraction, g-free. CONFIRMED [P-sketch] in its g-freeness, with two corrections.
 - The fixed-point map T of (1.13) has its nonlinear part K_0^{−1}P_0H*(δV/δA)(HB′). (98) [V] bounds δV/δA, not its derivative. A Lipschitz bound needs a Cauchy step: δV/δA is analytic on the a_3 ball and ≤ C_4(2ρ)² on the 2ρ ball, so its derivative is ≤ 4C_4ρ on the ρ ball (ρ ≤ a_3/2). So the constant is 4C_4, not 2C_4. CORRECTED (K2), a number only.
 - The ball radius is ρ ≈ 2‖K_0^{−1}‖B_3²4ε ∝ γ_0^{−1}M⁵ε. The Lipschitz constant is ‖K_0^{−1}‖·‖H‖²·4C_4ρ ∝ γ_0^{−2}M^{10}ε times norm-change powers of M. So ε_C = c(d, L)γ_0²M^{−p_C}, not c(d, L)γ_0M^{−p_C}. CORRECTED (K1). γ_0 is a fixed number of N-060, so the conclusion "no g" stands.
 - The term (δV/δA)(H_{1,k}B′) lives on Z, not only on Λ. A sum over Z could carry diam Z ∝ R_k. It does not, because H_{1,k}B′ decays like e^{−δ dist(·, Λ)} (CMP 102 (190) type decay, R-019). This step is not written in the file. I accept it as [P-sketch] given R-019. Balaban's printed bound (B_3²4ε_k, no R_k) on p. 359 [V] agrees.
C3. (H) Hessian, the only g-dependence. CONFIRMED [V + P]. Note on V8: in the proof Balaban applies (1.9) at the fixed extension with ζ_0 = 1. At general ε that extension has curvature O(1)B_3M²ε on Z [V p. 193]. A representation on Z (size 100MR_k [V p. 177]) gives \|A\| ≤ O(1)M³R_kε. This is below the file's M⁶R_kε. ## 6. Reading 1.5: where R_k in (1.7) comes from

What the images show.
- p. 357 [V]: Balaban applies Sect. F [15] to U_0 and represents it "on the domain Z as exp iξA_0" with \|A_0\|, \|∇^ηA_0\| < O(1)M⁶R_kε_k.
- p. 177 [V]: every component of Z lies in a cube of size 100MR_k (condition (i)).
- p. 195 [V], (1.80): \|U_0(∂p) − 1\| < 2ε_kη² + O(1)B_3B_5M⁵exp(−δ dist(p, Λ))ε_kη² on Ω_k. There is no R_k in this curvature bound.
- p. 359 [V]: the proof uses the χ_{k,Λ} extension, with \|∂U_{k,Z} − 1\| < 2ε_kη² (1.75). It does not use the Ω″_k bound O(R_kε_k) of p. 193.
Arithmetic. A gauge representation over a domain of diameter D of a configuration with curvature κ gives \|A\| ≤ O(D·κ). With D = 100MR_k (Z) and κ = O(M⁵ε_k) from (1.80), this is O(M⁶R_kε_k), the printed bound exactly. Reading (b) would need curvature O(R_kε_k) near Λ; (1.80) excludes this on Ω_k, and the proof does not use the Ω″_k bound. Also, under (b), a representation "on the domain Z" would carry a second factor from diam Z.
Decision. The images support reading (a) (diam Z) and give no support to (b). The origin is still not printed in words. Grade: (a) [V-supported inference]; (b) UNSUPPORTED. Consequence: route 4.3(b) to the strict (A-σ*) fails. The local route 4.3(a) (localized Sect. F and Sect. B) is unprinted. (A-σ*) stays NOT PROVED. The file already says it is not needed, and C15 confirms that.
Note. A sharper reading of (1.80) splits the curvature into a uniform part 2ε_kη² and a part B_5M⁵ε_k that decays away from Λ. Then \|A_0\| ≤ O(MR_kε_k + M⁶ε_k/δ). The R_k factor stays (from the uniform part), but with M¹ in place of M⁶. This does not remove R_k, so it does not change any conclusion here.
