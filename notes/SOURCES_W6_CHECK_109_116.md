## 3. Source-entry places on these pages that the L1_E list does not name

None of these is a danger-class entry, that is, a lower bound, division or logarithm from positivity that is used at complex input and is not handled by (D-i)–(D-iv), Lemma J3 or K-014. Item N2 adds one smallness condition.

- N1. CMP 116 pp. 12–13, (2.5)–(2.7). Balaban conditions on Z_0^c. He divides by the Gaussian integral ∫dB\|_{Z_0} exp(−⟨Z_0^cB′, C*Δ_kCZ_0B⟩ − ½⟨Z_0B, C*Δ_kCZ_0B⟩). This produces the Dirichlet covariance C^{(k)}(Z_0) and (C^{(k)})^{1/2}, through the resolvent integral (2.7) (C*Δ_kC)^{−1/2} = π^{−1}∫dx x^{−1/2}(xI + C*Δ_kC)^{−1}. With the source, f enters through Δ_k → C_f^{−1}. Class: (D-i) Gaussian normalization plus (S-c). SOURCES_M1_SWEEP lists these objects (Q9 conditioning, "(C_f)^{1/2} (x-integral of covariant resolvents)"), so row R-016 covers them. L1_E §4.1 (D-i) cites only det(C_f/C_0) and does not name this divisor.
- N2. CMP 116 pp. 15–17 and p. 20, (2.15)–(2.17), (2.23)–(2.26). Balaban replaces the operators by those at σ(Z) = 0, U = U, J = 0. The error forms R_1, R_2, R_3 have matrix elements ≤ (O(1)e^{−δ_0M/3} + O(α_0 + α_1))exp(−½δ_0\|b_− − b′_−\|) (2.16). The determinant ratio gives exp((O(1)e^{−δ_0M/3} + O(α_0 + α_1))\|Z_0\|) (2.17), and (2.24)–(2.25) give exp O(α_5)\|Z\|, where α_5 = O(1)e^{−δ_0M/3} + O(α_0 + α_1) + O(1)α_4 + γ_2. p. 20 then assumes "(LM)⁴α_0, (LM)⁴α_1, (LM)⁴α_4, (LM)⁴γ_2 are bounded by … 1". (2.24) also assumes α_5‖C^{(k)}(Z_0, 0)‖ < ½. At complex source the non-real part of C_f^{−1} must go into R_1–R_3, so α_5 gains a term of the size of that perturbation. The source then needs (LM)⁴·(source part of α_5) ≤ O(1) and α_5‖C‖ < ½. Both are upper-bound conditions, and they hold if the source part is O(γ²r) with γ chosen after L and M. But L1_E (F4) states only τ ≤ ½, and condition (J-c) does not contain this (LM)⁴ condition. This is a new item for the condition list. It is not a danger entry.
- N3. CMP 116 p. 16, (2.22). χ_{k,Y_0}(B)χ^c_{k,P}(B) ≤ exp(−½γ_2(ε_1²/g_k²)\|P\| + ½γ_2‖PB‖²). The threshold ε_1/g_k uses the reference g_k (N2 of L1_E). The ‖PB‖² term is absorbed into the Gaussian, which needs the lower form bound on Re C_f^{−1} (L1_E (F4), (S-c)). This is covered but not named.
- N4. CMP 116 p. 21. log Z^{(k)}(U_{k+1}) − log Z^{(k)}(1) is localized by the generalized random walk expansion of [16] (63). E_0 for these terms is redefined: "We define ½E_0 as equal to this constant", with δ_0M ≥ κ. With the source this is the f-dependent Gaussian normalization (D-i)/(F4). It is covered generically but not named.
- N5. CMP 109 pp. 266–268, (2.10)–(2.12). In the small-field step the gauge-fixing weight enters as −(1/g_k²)G(B′). Its quadratic part G^{(2)} is inside Δ^{(k)} ((2.11), "δ-function gauge fixing term replaced by the exponential one"), and its cubic part (1/g_k²)G_3(g_kCB) is inside P^{(k)} ((2.12)). With the adapted weight (K-011; SOURCES_D3 (vi)), f enters C_f through G^{(2)} and P_f through G_3. SOURCES_D3 (vi)(e) and (F3)/(S-d) cover both. L1_E §4.1 (F2) describes only the one-link normalization z(w) of the CMP 122 I form (with χ(\|u − 1\| < ε_j)), and (F4) writes C_f with the Wilson source part 𝒱_S only. The (F2)/(F4) text should name the gauge-fixing part.
- N6. CMP 109 (2.13), p. 268. E^{(k+1)} = log∫dμ_{C^{(k)}}χ_k exp[…] is a logarithm of an integral of a positive density (at real input). It is the CMP 109 analogue of (D-iv). It is handled by the CMP 116 (2.12)–(2.13) series and Lemma J3, as L1_E §4.1 states generically. It is not named by page.
