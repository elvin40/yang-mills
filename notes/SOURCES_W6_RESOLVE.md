### 1.2 Lemma G: the inequality of (vi)(d) follows from the printed (1.6) [P]
Setting. G = SU(N) (or U(N)), tr = (1/N)Tr, |·| the Hilbert–Schmidt norm, n = dim G. Sourced form of (1.6) under K-011: the factor z(w)^{−1}exp[−w(1 − Re tr u)], with a weight w = w_j(y) in place of g_j^{−2}. Let z(w) := ∫du e^{−w(1 − Re tr u)}χ(|u − 1| < ε_j) (the D3 (vi)(c) definition), or the same integral without χ. Let w_r > 0 be real with w_rε_j² ≥ 1, and let w ∈ C with Re w ≥ (1 − θ)w_r, 0 ≤ θ ≤ ½, and |w − w_r| ≤ 3r with r ≤ 1/8.
Claim. For every u with |u − 1| ≥ ε_j:
  |z(w)^{−1}e^{−w(1 − Re tr u)}| ≤ 2C_G w_r^{n/2} exp(−(1 − θ)w_rε_j²/(2N)).
Proof. Step 1. For u ∈ U(N) with eigenvalues e^{iφ_k}, 1 − (1/N)Re Tr u = (1/N)Σ_k 2sin²(φ_k/2) and |u − 1|² = Σ_k 4sin²(φ_k/2). So 1 − Re tr u = |u − 1|²/(2N) exactly. Hence |e^{−w(1 − Re tr u)}| = e^{−Re w·|u−1|²/(2N)} ≤ exp(−(1 − θ)w_rε_j²/(2N)) when |u − 1| ≥ ε_j.
Step 2. Lower bound on z(w_r). Put ρ := w_r^{−1/2} ≤ ε_j. On the ball |u − 1| ≤ ρ, χ = 1 and w_r(1 − Re tr u) ≤ 1/(2N). So z(w_r) ≥ e^{−1/(2N)}·Haar{|u − 1| ≤ ρ} ≥ C_G^{−1}ρ^n = C_G^{−1}w_r^{−n/2}. The Haar volume of a ball of radius ρ ≤ 2 is ≥ cρ^n, because the exponential map is a diffeomorphism near 0 with Jacobian bounded below [P-sketch: standard].
Step 3. Complex w. z(w)/z(w_r) = E_r[e^{−(w − w_r)(1 − Re tr u)}], with E_r the probability measure z(w_r)^{−1}e^{−w_r(1 − Re tr u)}χ du.
 (χ-version of z.) On supp χ, 0 ≤ 1 − Re tr u ≤ ε_j²/(2N). So |e^{−(w−w_r)(1−Re tr u)} − 1| ≤ e^{3rε_j²} − 1 ≤ ½ once 3rε_j² ≤ log(3/2), which holds for g_j small (ε_j → 0). Hence |z(w)/z(w_r) − 1| ≤ ½ and |z(w)|^{−1} ≤ 2z(w_r)^{−1}.
 (χ-free version of z.) The global bound 0 ≤ 1 − Re tr u ≤ 2 gives only |… − 1| ≤ e^{6r} − 1, which is not < ½. So split E_r at |u − 1| = ε_j. The inner part is bounded as in the χ-version. The outer part has E_r-mass ≤ C_Gw_r^{n/2}e^{−w_rε_j²/(2N)} (Steps 1–2), and its integrand has modulus ≤ e^{6r} ≤ e^{3/4}. For g_j small (w_rε_j² large compared with log w_r) the outer contribution is ≤ ¼. So |z(w)/z(w_r) − 1| ≤ ¾ and |z(w)|^{−1} ≤ 4z(w_r)^{−1}; replace 2 by 4 in the claim. ∎
Corollary. With w_r = g_j^{−2}(y)(local coupling) and ε_j²/g_j² large compared with log g_j^{−2}, the right side is ≤ exp(−γ_0(1 − θ)ε_j²/g_j²) for any γ_0 < 1/(2N) and g_j small. This is the D3 (vi)(d) inequality, with (1 − θ) in place of (1 − 3γ²r) (the D3_VERIFY V8 correction) and γ_0 ≤ 1/(2N) made explicit. [P]

## 2. Point 2: which R-denominator construction is live

### 2.1 Two meanings of "source-free" [P, from the cited texts]
The files use "source-free" in two different senses.
- (s1) Activity-free: the denominator contains no E-, R- or B-activity, only a Wilson action, χ, δ and Haar factors. This is the claim of SOURCES_B20_W4R §1.3 ("It contains NO activity E, R, or B"), SOURCES_FX_O23 §3, and L1_E (D-iii) ("they contain the bare Wilson action only"). It is printed: II (1.1) p. 356 and I (1.100) p. 201 [V, this session].
- (s2) Weight-free: the Wilson weight of the denominator does not depend on f. This is "choice A" of SOURCES_FX_O23 §4. The opposite, "choice B", gives the denominator (and the matching front factor) the sourced weight w = ωg_k^{−2}. Choice B is D3 (P4), SOURCES_D3_NEW4 §1, §3, and L-165 Step 1.
Balaban's pages cannot decide (s2). His construction has f = 0, so his constant g_k is both the reference and the local weight on Z. "The printed (1.1), (1.100) are source-free" is true in sense (s1) only.
L1_E (D-iii) is choice B in substance: its bound uses σ_W, "the source perturbation with |σ_W| ≤ ‖ζ‖_∞Φ(γ)" (L-165 Steps 4–5). A weight-free denominator has no σ_W. So L1_E and D3 (P4) do not disagree on the construction. They disagree only in wording. SOURCES_W6_CHECK_122 §1 row (1.10)–(1.11) and §5 read L1_E's "bare Wilson action only" in sense (s2); that reading is not what L1_E uses.

### 2.2 The normalization identity holds for either weight [P]
In I (1.100) the front factor is χ_{k,Λ_i}δ_{G_i}(V′_k)χ(Λ_i)exp[−g_k^{−2}A(ζ_i, U_{k,X_i}(V′_kV_{Λ_i}))] and the denominator is its integral over V′_k [V p. 201]. ### 2.3 Decision: choice B is the live construction
Reason 1 (the reset). II p. 356 [V]: "A part of this term from the nominator has to be combined with the term in the first exponential, to give the Wilson term of the new action. The remaining part has to be cancelled, or approximately cancelled, with the term from the denominator." So the Wilson weight of the new action on Z is the weight of the front factor. The project's sourced hypothesis needs that weight to be u_k(f(x)) on Z: D3 (1.a) and (P3), the reset (B10′) "w_k(x) := u_k(f(x)) for x ∈ Z", and the pointwise flow w_k = u_k(f(x)) on the last domain that D2C's extraction uses. Under choice A the front factor carries g_k^{−2}, so the new weight on Z is g_k^{−2}, not u_k(f(x)). Then D3 (1.a) is false on Z, and the difference −Σ_{p⊂Z}(u_k(f(p)) − g_k^{−2})P_p(U_k) must be carried as an activity. That term is marginal and has size O(r)P_p on every plaquette of Z. It is not of R-class size g^{κ_0}, and it is not localized at a boundary. Its bookkeeping is Q-061, graded "plausible, not checked against later steps" (SOURCES_FX_O23_VERIFY, quoted in SOURCES_FX_D §1). [P given p. 356 and D3 (1.a)]
Reason 2 (the proved lemmas). Every lemma that bounds the denominator at complex source uses choice B: L-165 Step 1 ("The source enters only through the weight w_m"); SOURCES_D3_NEW4 §1, §4 (Hessian Δ_1(ζ_0ω)); L1_E (D-iii) (σ_W); FABLE_O3_ALC Lemma O3-den (D_k(ω; U), unverified).

Every consumer of the denominator already carries R-042 (L1_E §7 input list; R-043). So choice A saves no input and adds Q-061.
Decision. Choice B (sourced weight ωg_k^{−2} in the front factor and in the denominator; no activity in either) is the live construction for the chain L1_E → L-323, L-165 → R-043. Q-061 is not on the chain under choice B. [P given Reasons 1–3]
