## §0 Result

The [I-strong] step of L1_B §6.4 is closed relative to R-018. The R-operation background is, by Balaban's own definition, the CMP 102 minimal configuration for a determining set of the term (G1, G10). CMP 102 Theorem 1 takes regularity of the DATA as its hypothesis, not regularity of the minimizer (G7). The data on every component of the R-operation determining set are bounded by printed characteristic functions and by a printed inductive assumption (Lemma D). The mixed domain sequence is admissible (Lemma A). So Theorem 1 gives the global bound on Ω_{j+1} ∩ Z directly (Theorem R (b)). On the Gaussian layer itself the printed χ^{(n+1)} and a one-line extension of Balaban's (1.46) give a sharper bound with constant c ∈ {1, 3} (Theorem R (a)).
The circularity found by L1_B_VERIFY §6.4 is real for the route "(3.17)–(3.19) at level j" alone. Balaban's printed step-k order is different: first (3.12), "the restrictions on the fields V_k, V_{k+1} … and Theorem 1 imply" global regularity; then (3.17) uses it (G5, G6). The repair here follows that order. It does not need (3.17)–(3.19) at all.
Both readings (R-a), (R-b) of the Gaussian background (L1_B_VERIFY §6) are covered, because both backgrounds are minimal configurations U(𝔹, V) for determining sets of the term.

 Ω^{(n)}_i := Ω″_i for i ≤ j, Ω^{(n)}_i := Ω_i for i ≥ j + 1 (inside Z; Ω_i outside Z) [P from (1.12), (1.16)].
