## §0 Result

(a) R_0 is not printed anywhere. CMP 96 p. 224 says "R is a big positive integer which will be fixed later", and CMP 96 never fixes it. CMP 99 p. 408 says only "a pair R_0, M_0 of smallest possible numbers". CMP 99 Regular p. 98 says "R_1, M_1 are smallest integers for which all the theorems of the papers [2, 4] are valid". So no file and no page bounds R_0, and this file does not bound it either. The task is settled the other way. R_0 enters the proofs of CMP 99 Thms 3.1–3.3 only through the local □-sequences of p. 408, which are built at block R_0M_0 with spacing 2R_0M_0. These sequences meet the R_0 condition by construction. The global sequence enters only through its layer widths W_j (CMP 96 Lemma 2.1, (2.57)–(2.59)) and through the cube geometry of §C. Both are covered by (RB) and by M-thresholds. So the entry M ≥ R_0M_1 of SOURCES_ROP_GEOMETRY_VERIFY is not load-bearing [P-sketch, §2]. If a reader keeps Balaban's general phrase "R sufficiently large" (CMP 99 Regular p. 77) as a hypothesis, the canon can still fix R_0 before M: R_0 depends on (d, L) only and sits at N-060, so M > R_0M_1 at N-310 is a lower bound from earlier lines and cannot create a cycle [P]. §6 books it as an optional safety entry.
(b) Balaban himself applies the CMP 99 Propagators theorems at a big block M_1 with layer separation RM_1 (CMP 99 Regular p. 77, (1.4) [V]), and he places the (3.35) class at the block R_1M_1 (p. 98 [V]). So the M_1 reading of (iv) is Balaban's own use. §3 lists the 17 places where the block size enters the proofs of Thms 3.1–3.3 (pp. 396–416 [V]). Each place becomes an M_1-threshold (fixed at N-270), a case of ROP Lemma CC′ with s ≤ 18 (covered by (RB)), or a W-threshold (an M-threshold). The amplitude conditions do not change: Balaban uses "Mα_0 ≤ a_0" only as the bound of A on the one cube that holds Ω_0(□) (Cor. 3.6), and that is the canon cap L²b ≤ a_1. All five consumers get what they cite (§3.3) [P-sketch].

## §2 Task (a): R_0

2.1 What R_0 is [V G1, G7, G13, G14]. R_0 is Balaban's least R for the proofs of CMP 96 (CMP 99 p. 408). It is a power of L. Its value is never printed. The conditions behind it are stated with constants of (d, L) only (G2: δ_0, c_0; G3: cube counts), so R_0 = R_0(d, L) [I, strong: CMP 99 Regular p. 77 says the same of M_1, "it depends on d and L only"]. The canon already uses R_0 inside the product R_0M_0 at N-060.

2.2 No bound from print [V]. The only R-conditions that I can read are geometric (CMP 96 p. 238: the torus □̃³, side 8 big blocks, carries "only two scales") or RM-products ((2.59)). A Lemma-CC count for □̃³ needs R > 8dL in the ℓ¹ form. But "smallest possible numbers satisfying conditions needed in the proofs" also covers CMP 95, CMP 89 and CMP 96 §C–§D, which I did not read in full.
