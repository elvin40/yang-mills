## Euclidean invariance + Balaban scope (lit, 2026-10-27, agent Opus unless [V])
- Lattice limits give only hypercubic symmetry + reflection positivity. Three known mechanisms for full O(4): (a) identify with a rotation-invariant continuum regularization; (b) orientation-independence of the limit, which needs UNIQUENESS of the limit, not compactness (BFS-II 1980 suggested "lattices of varying orientation"); (c) uniqueness of invariant measure of a rotation-covariant dynamic (Duch–Hairer–Yi–Zhao arXiv:2508.07776 Thm 2.18 for Φ⁴₃, small coupling). Route (c) is unavailable in 4D YM: the Langevin dynamic is critical, regularity structures stop at d=3 (CCHS 2022). Route (a) fails: MRS 1993 is axial gauge, "not Euclidean invariant", no OS, IR cutoff never lifted, gauge-fixed correlators only, Slavnov identities = main result. → Route (b) or a new idea is forced. Jaffe–Witten fn. 2 forbids compactness alone, but uniqueness of the limit would satisfy fn. 2 and route (b) at once.
- Balaban 4D (CMP 109, 116, 119, 122): effective-action densities incl. large fields on a torus, bounds uniform in spacing; no observables (MRS: his UV limit "not necessarily unique"). Jaffe–Witten [V]: "need to be extended to the study of expectations of gauge-invariant functions".

- Nontriviality: no lattice gauge construction in d≥3 has a nontrivial limit (Chatterjee [V]); Φ⁴₃ non-Gaussianity proofs use superrenormalizability.
