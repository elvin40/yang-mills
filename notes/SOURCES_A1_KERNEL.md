Key structural fact [P, from the above]: the proof never compares U with 1 globally. A regular non-abelian U is close to a pure gauge only on each cube of size O(M) blocks; the holonomy over large loops is not small. So the brief's plan (3), a global factorization (QGQ*)^{−1}(U) = (QGQ*)^{−1}(1)[1 + R(U)]^{−1}, is NOT available (there is no global gauge with U^u ≈ 1, and the operators for U and 1 do not act compatibly far apart). ## 5. Geometry of the multiscale lattice [V CMP 96 pp. 231, 234–235 images/text]
𝔅 = ∪_{j=0}^k Λ_j (2.45). d(y,y′) (2.46) = infimum over admissible contours from y to y′ of the length measured in local units: a bond inside B^j(Λ_j) counts (L^jη)^{−1}·η [V text: "infimum is taken over all admissible contours"; formula itself garbled, I]. An infimum of path lengths over a class closed under concatenation is a metric, so d obeys the triangle inequality [I; Lemma 2.1 (2.63) uses it].
Lemma 2.1 [V p. 234]: for 0 < α < 1, c_1(α) = 12c_0^d(½α), and RM satisfying (2.59) (δ_0RM > 16d log c_0 + 1 at α = ½):
 (2.60) e^{−αδ_0 d(y,y′)} ≤ e^{−αδ_0RM max{|j−j′|−1, 0}}, y ∈ Λ_j, y′ ∈ Λ_{j′};
 (2.61) sup_y Σ_{y′∈𝔅} e^{−αδ_0 d(y,y′)} ≤ c_1(α); (2.63) chained sums ≤ c_1(α)^n e^{−(1−α)δ_0 d(y,y′)}.
Hilbert space [V p. 235 (2.69)]: ⟨λ, λ′⟩ = Σ_j Σ_{y∈Λ_j} (L^jη)^d λ(y)λ′(y). Write μ(y) := (L^jη)^d, s(y) := (L^jη)², for y ∈ Λ_j. Kernels are taken with respect to μ: (Tf)(y) = Σ_{y′} μ(y′)t(y,y′)f(y′).
Scale-power conversion [P from (2.60)]: for y ∈ Λ_j, y′ ∈ Λ_{j′}, and any real p, L^{p|j−j′|} ≤ L^{|p|} e^{(|p| log L/(RM)) d(y,y′)}. So the split of a total power between (L^jη) and (L^{j′}η) costs a decay rate |p| log L/(RM) (Balaban's "conventional" remark, CMP 99 p. 398, CMP 96 p. 235 under (2.68)). Define θ := log L/(RM); θ is small when RM is large. [I: (2.60) with α = 1 is assumed; Balaban states α < 1, so use α close to 1 and absorb.]
Schur constant [P given (2.60)–(2.61)]: Σ(δ′) := sup_y Σ_{y′} (μ(y′)/μ(y))^{1/2}(1 + d(y,y′)) e^{−δ′d(y,y′)}. Since (μ(y′)/μ(y))^{1/2} ≤ L^{d/2}e^{(d/2)θ d(y,y′)} and (1 + x)e^{−x/4·δ′} ≤ 4/(eδ′) + 1, Σ(δ′) ≤ L^{d/2}(1 + 4/(eδ′)) c_1 at rate δ′/2 − (d/2)θ, finite when δ′ > dθ and that rate is admissible in (2.61). Σ(δ′) depends on d, L, δ′ only; it is uniform in k, η, and the sequence {Ω_j} [R: Lemma 2.1].
