## Q4. Geometry recomputed under both readings [P + numerics]
- ℓ^∞ reading, by hand. (4.1) A point x with d_∞(x, Z_0) ≤ M lies in a grid M-cube whose index differs by ≤ 1 per coordinate from the cube of the nearest z ∈ Z_0, so that cube touches Z_0: Z̃_0 ⊇ N_M(Z_0). Cubes outside Z̃_0 do not touch Z_0, so they are ≥ one full cube away: d_∞(Z̃_0^c, Z_0) ≥ M. CONFIRMED.
- (4.2) X_0 := M_1-cubes whose 11^d block lies in Z̃_0. In each M-cube (q = M/M_1 cubes per side), indices 5..q−6 are in X_0 and every index 0..q−1 is within 5 of that range iff q ≥ 11, i.e. q > 10. Dilation never leaves Z̃_0 by definition. So X̃_0^5 = Z̃_0. X_0^c lies within 5M_1 of Z̃_0^c, so d_∞(X_0^c, Z_0) ≥ M − 5M_1. CONFIRMED.
- (4.3) Non-near X has a point within M_1 of X_0^c, so a point at d_∞ ≥ M − 6M_1 from Z_0; with ℓ^∞-diameter ≤ c_XM_1, d_∞(X, Z_0) ≥ M − (6 + c_X)M_1. This is > ⅔M iff q > 3(6 + c_X). MINOR CORRECTION: the author writes "≥ ⅔M iff q ≥ 3(6 + c_X)"; p. 13 asks strict ">"; use q > 3(6 + c_X) (or keep ≥ and note the author's first inequality is strict only if the M_1-margin is strict). Euclidean dist ≥ d_∞, so the bound transfers. Near domains satisfy X̃^5 ⊂ X̃_0^5 = Z̃_0 (dilation is monotone), so merging keeps the U-dependence inside Z̃_0. CONFIRMED.
- Numerics (own script, numerics/wc_residue_geom.py; d = 2, q = 16, L-shaped Z_0 with convex and concave corners): ℓ^∞: X̃_0^5 = Z̃_0 True; min index gap X_0^c to Z_0 = 12 (= distance 11M_1 = M − 5M_1); Z_0-cubes outside X_0 = 0. Face-only: X̃_0^5 = Z̃_0 FALSE for the eroded X_0; gap 0; 50 M_1-cubes of Z_0 outside X_0. Both match the author's claims.
- Face-only reading (author 4.5): CONFIRMED. At a convex corner of Z_0 the diagonal M-cube is outside Z̃_0 and shares a vertex with Z_0: separation 0. The M_1-cube of Z_0 at the corner is at ℓ^1 index distance 2 ≤ 5 from that diagonal cube, so it cannot be in X_0; it carries nonzero diagonal kernel of C^{(k)}(Z_0), and CMP 99 p. 413 says R(X) has kernel support in X × X; so some domain X ∌ X_0 with dist(X, Z_0) = 0 is needed. p. 13 contradicts itself under this reading.
- ADDITION (stronger than the author): under face-only adjoining, X̃_0^5 = Z̃_0 has no solution at all whenever Z̃_0 has a convex corner. Reason: a corner M_1-cube of Z̃_0 can be reached by a 5-step face-dilation only from a cube at ℓ^1 index distance ≤ 5; the 5-step face-dilation of that cube is an ℓ^1 ball that leaves the box quadrant unless its centre is ≥ 5 from both faces, i.e. at ℓ^1 distance ≥ 10. So the defining equation of X_0 on p. 13 is unsatisfiable. Under ℓ^∞ it is satisfiable (q > 10). Part B: CONFIRMED.

## Q6. Lemma CP and Lemma AW, re-derived [P]
- Lemma CP, own proof. f(s) := F(U(s)) is analytic on |s| < t (U(s) is linear in (A′, J) through e^{isL^{−1}ηA′}, tJ; Thm 3.4 gives analyticity in A′), ‖f‖ ≤ Γ. Operator-valued Cauchy on the disc of radius t − |s|: ‖f′(s)‖ ≤ Γ/(t − |s|). Integrate on [0, 1]: ‖f(1) − f(0)‖ ≤ Γ log(t/(t − 1)) ≤ Γ/(t − 1) (log(1 + x) ≤ x, x = 1/(t − 1)). With r := 1/t this is Γ·r/(1 − r), the form in the brief. Γ/(t − 1) ≤ ½c iff t ≥ 1 + 2Γ/c. Then Re⟨u, f(1)u⟩ ≥ Re⟨u, f(0)u⟩ − ‖f(1) − f(0)‖‖u‖² ≥ ½c‖u‖². CONFIRMED. (Alternative via Schwarz's lemma: ‖F(z) − F(0)‖ ≤ 2Γ|z|/ρ, condition t ≥ 4Γ/c; the author's condition is the better one for Γ/c > ½.)
- MINOR CORRECTION to Lemma CP's hypothesis. The author states "F(U_c^0) real symmetric, ≥ c".
