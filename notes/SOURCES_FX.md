## 3. CMP 116 §2: the cluster-expansion machinery and its convergence criterion [V, text layer lines 366–1040]
- Polymer gas (2.11) [V]: "(2.1) = Σ H(Z_1)…H(Z_n) = 1 + Σ …", with "ζ(Z, Z′) = 0 if Z ∩ Z′ contains a cube, or a wall of a cube, and ζ(Z, Z′) = 1 otherwise." Factorization (2.10): H(Z) factorizes over the connected components of Z.
- Exponentiation [V]: "If the activities H(Z) of the above polymer expansion are sufficiently small, then the polymer expansion can be exponentiated according to the well-known formula, see [36,60,26,25,67,50]." (2.12)–(2.13): E^{(k+1)}(X) = Σ_n (1/n!) Σ_{(Z_1,…,Z_n): ∪Z_i = X} ρ^T(Z_1,…,Z_n) H(Z_1)…H(Z_n), "ρ^T(Z) = 1, and ρ^T(Z_1,…,Z_n) = Σ_{Γ∈C_n} Π (ζ(Z_i,Z_j) − 1)", C_n = connected graphs. This is the Ursell (truncated) function form; supports of the output terms are the unions X = ∪Z_i, connected.
- Convergence criterion actually used, Lemma 3 (2.38) [V]: "|H(Z)| ≤ C_3 ε_1 exp(−(1−8δ)½Lκ d_{k+1}(Z))", then "The above lemma implies that sufficient conditions for convergence of the series (2.12), (2.13) are satisfied, see [26,67,25,50]." and (2.39)–(2.40): "To the above sum we can repeat all the considerations and bounds of the paper [26], for κ sufficiently large, and ε_1 sufficiently small." Output (2.41): "|E^{(k+1)}(X)| ≤ O(1)C_3 ε_1 exp(−(1−10δ)½Lκ d_{k+1}(X))". Entropy input (1.28): Σ over domains with fixed tree length ≤ exp(O(1)·d_k(Y)) (formula partly garbled).
- [26] in the CMP 109/116 list = "Cammarota, C.: Decay of correlations for infinite range interactions in unbounded spin systems" (CMP 85, 1982) [V title; volume from memory]; [25] = "Brydges, D.: A short course on cluster expansions" (Les Houches 1984). CMP 122 II's "standard proof … (e.g., in [26])" most likely means the same Cammarota paper [inference: CMP 122 has no own list; [I]'s list has [26] = Cammarota].
- So the criterion Balaban uses everywhere is the Cammarota form: |activity(Z)| ≤ small × e^{−κ d(Z)} with κ beating the tree-graph entropy of (1.28). This is the criterion (FX) inherits; FX3–FX4 are its restatement (Kotecký–Preiss with a(Γ) = c|Γ| is an equivalent sufficient condition for this decay; not needed separately).

## 6. Construction of (FX): a final R-step plus a unit-scale cluster expansion

### 6.1 Step A — final R-operation with Z″ = ∅ [R to Balaban's own bounds; one new check]
At scale K no later steps exist, so no component needs to keep its factor. Apply (0.3) with every remaining component in Z′ (Z″ = ∅). By (0.5) with Z″ = ∅ this is Σ_{Z′} ∫dV_{Z′}ρ(Z′,V)/∫dV_{Z′}ρ(∅,V) = exp Σ_X R_fin(X,V). Normalization (0.4)/(1.102) keeps ∫dV_K ρ_K exact (D4.1(f), VERIFY §6).
- Polymer = a connected union U of: final large-field components Y (each with its whole fine history, i.e. Balaban's T′_K(Y)), Mayer links e^{V(X)} − 1 of the coupling terms V(X) (localization domains X, CMP 122 II (1.72)), and boundary terms. This is CMP 122 II (1.90) and Dimock III (132)–(134). Factorization over disconnected U is exact: T-operations factorize over components ((2.19) [III], VERIFY §2) and the Mayer expansion removes the couplings.
- Activity bound (i): by (1.73) and (1.79)–(1.80), Σ_{histories→Y} |T′_K(Y)(…)| ≤ exp(−κ_K(Y) − 2p_0(g_{j(Y)})) · Π(Mayer-link bounds). κ_K(Y) ≥ Σ_n O(1)M^dR_n^d d′_n(S^{n−K}(Y)) ≥ c M^dR_K^d d′_K(Y) gives tree decay at the unit scale; 2p_0(g_{j(Y)}) ≥ 2p_0(γ) gives smallness. With Dimock's Lemma 16 (214) for the tree length of a union, |K(U)| ≤ e^{−p_0(γ)} e^{−κ′ d_K(U)}. This is the Cammarota/CMP 116 (2.38) criterion; exponentiation then follows as in (2.12)–(2.13) [V] and Dimock III Thm 2 [V].
- New check needed (A1): (1.80) is stated as a budget for "K renormalization steps" still to come; at the last scale only the first term (n = K+1) is needed, as tree decay. Balaban does not state the n = K+1 term as a lower bound on κ_K for non-renormalizable Y; it follows from (1.80) as printed if κ_K is defined for all components, which (1.85) does. [inference]
- Correction to the brief (i): CMP 122 I (0.6) is not a convergence statement; the convergence of Σ_geometries is (1.79)–(1.89) plus (1.97) inside each R step, and CMP 122 II (0.1) globally. Balaban's (0.1) alone (Σ ≤ e^{E_+|T|}) is NOT enough: polymer convergence needs a SMALL per-polymer activity with tree decay, which is (1.79)–(1.80), not (0.1).

### 6.2 Step B — the unit-scale small-field integral [R to CMP 116 §2 / Dimock III]
After Step A, I_K[f] = ∫dV_K χ exp(A_K^f + Σ_X R_fin(X)). This is a small-field integral on the unit lattice. Its log is a sum of local terms by the "final localization by a cluster expansion" of CMP 116 §2 ((2.9)–(2.13), Lemma 3 (2.38)), as Dimock III Thm 2 does for φ⁴₃ (Z = Z(0) exp Σ_X H(X)). Then log I_K[f] − log I_K[0] = Σ_X [H(X;f) − H(X;0)]. Together with W_K (S1(b), already local) and the R-terms in the action (already local, S1(c)), this gives the polymer families P_m of (FX), with m = the coarsest scale of each term, (FX1) by construction and (FX3)–(FX4) from (2.38)/(2.41) and (1.28).

### 6.3 Step C — local holomorphy radius (FX2) [R, one conditional item]

### 6.4 Step D — ε-uniformity (iv) [P, given A–C]
All activity bounds are in units of cubes of the polymer's coarsest scale, with constants (p_0(γ), κ, c r) independent of K and ε.
