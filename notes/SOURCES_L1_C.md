 Printed definitions [V CMP 98 p. 19]. The linear average (14): "Ā_c = Σ_{x∈B(c_−)} L^{−(d+1)}(A(Γ_{c_−,x}) + A([x, x(c)]) + A(Γ_{x(c),c_+}))", where Γ_{c_−,x} runs in B(c_−) and Γ_{x(c),c_+} in B(c_+).  (a) Block-locality [P]. In (14), (15), (0.12) the coarse bond c uses only bonds of B(c_−) ∪ B(c_+) and the corridor bonds [x, x(c)] between them. The k-step average Q_k is the composition of one-step averages (CMP 98, the setting of (10)–(15) [V p. 19]: "U … on Ω^{(j)}, V … on Ω^{(j+1)}"). So Q and Q* are block-local with range one block pair. The same holds for Q(U) at a background U: the transports in (15), (0.12) use links inside the same blocks.
 (b) Normalization [P-sketch]. For the linearized one-step average, every coefficient is ≥ 0 and the coefficients of one coarse bond sum to an average of contour lengths divided by L: Σ_b|q(c, b)| ≤ L^{−(d+1)}·L^d·(2dL + 1) ≤ 2d + 1. In the inner products of CMP 99 (block measure (L^jη)^d, bond measure η^d) this gives |(Q*1_{y′})(b)| ≤ c(d, L) for b in the block pair of y′, and 0 elsewhere.
