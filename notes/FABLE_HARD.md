### 2.2 Where the limit's ℓ-dependence sits at separation d ≤ 1 [P given L3, L6, L14, as in FABLE (3.2)]
κ_B^∞ = −∂^B W_∞ + Σ_{π ⊢ B} κ^{ν_∞}(∂^{B_1}Δ_∞, …, ∂^{B_k}Δ_∞). For |B| = 2 at separation d:

### 3.4 The joint limit (K and ℓ together) [P]
Theorem IV's sequence is (K_i, ℓ_i). Non-uniqueness has two independent sources: the K-direction (Target 2's subsequence; E13, UV, open) and the ℓ-direction (§3.2, IR).
