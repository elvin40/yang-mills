## 2. (M-A′): the A_min bookkeeping
Notation. F(V′) = A(ζ_i, U_{k,X_i}(V′V_{Λ_i})) on the cutoff ball B (product of balls, MA §3). A_min = min_B F = F(X*), a number: it depends on V_{Λ_i} and on the exterior, not on V′. G = F − A_min ≥ 0. D^A(s) = ∫_B δχ e^{−sF} dV′, D^G(s) = ∫_B δχ e^{−sG} dV′. Then D^A(s) = e^{−sA_min}D^G(s) for every complex s. [P, trivial]
2.1 Normalized ratios see only G [P]. For every s with D^A(s) ≠ 0: e^{−sF}/D^A(s) = e^{−sG}/D^G(s). So Balaban's ratio at real u is N_u/D_u = e^{−uG}/D^G(u), and the frozen ratio is N_x/D_x = e^{−x^{−2}G}/D^G(x^{−2}). A_min cancels exactly in each. (1.102) holds for the frozen ratio as an exact identity at any complex u. [P]
2.2 How HHOL_LF §3.4(c) writes it [V, SOURCES_HHOL_LF §3.4(c)]. Quote: "the mismatch between the frozen ratio density (x_k^{−2}) and the action at u on Λ_i is the factor e^{−(u − x_k^{−2})A(ζ_i,·)}. It multiplies a positive integrand … the factor times the real density N_x/D_x integrates to D_x(x^{−2} − |δ|)/D_x(x^{−2})." So HHOL_LF forms N_x/D_x · e^{−(u−x^{−2})F} = e^{−uF}/D^A(x^{−2}). That is the UNNORMALIZED numerator at u over the normalizer at x. Its total mass is D^A(u)/D^A(x^{−2}) = e^{−(u−x^{−2})A_min}·D^G(u)/D^G(x^{−2}). The first factor has modulus up to e^{θx^{−2}A_min}. This is the step that mixes couplings: the A_min inside the numerator is at u, the A_min inside the normalizer is at x. [P, located]
2.3 The fix [P]. The chain after (1.100) extracts e^{−uF} into the new effective action A_k at u (p. 202 quote, §1). Write the frozen ratio as
  N_x/D_x = e^{−uF} · [e^{uA_min}/D^G(x^{−2})] · e^{(u−x^{−2})G}.   (2.1)
This is an algebraic identity (expand F = A_min + G). The three factors are:
 (a) e^{−uF}: the action at u on Λ_i, extracted into A_k. Same as Balaban's chain at coupling u.
 (b) e^{uA_min}/D^G(x^{−2}): V′-independent. In Balaban's ratio at real u the same slot holds 1/D_u = e^{uA_min}/D^G(u). So e^{uA_min} sits at the SAME coupling u as in Balaban's chain. It is entire and zero-free in u. It is not a mismatch. Only the G-normalizer is frozen: 1/D^G(x^{−2}) is real, positive, and g-free (a frozen normalization constant, the same convention as (M-D)).
 (c) e^{(u−x^{−2})G}: the true mismatch. It contains G, not F.
So the coupling-mixing factor e^{±(u−x^{−2})A_min} never appears if A_min is kept at u in both (a) and (b). The A_min in (a) and in (b) cancel inside (2.1), as they must, since the left side is A_min-free. [P]
2.4 Is A_min a constant on the ball? Yes. A_min = F(X*) is the value at the minimizer X* of F over B̄ (MA §3). It is a function of (V_{Λ_i}, exterior), not of V′. Note: X* is the minimizer of the (1.100) exponent with ζ_i on X_i. It need not equal V′ = 1, because Balaban's Prop. 1 minimizer V_Λ minimizes A(U_{k,Z}(·)) without ζ_i on Z (MA §2). The cancellation in 2.1 does not use where X* sits. [P]
2.5 Cost with the fix [P given (H−)]. Bound needed: the average of |mismatch| against the frozen density, with G.
 - HHOL_LF direction e^{−(u−x^{−2})G}, Re u ≥ (1−θ)x^{−2}, θ = 0.174: ≤ D^G((1−θ)x^{−2})/D^G(x^{−2}) ≤ e^{0.1906n_i(1+η_x)} (MA §6, exactly as stated there).
 - Direction (2.1)(c), e^{+(u−x^{−2})G}: |·| = e^{(Re u − x^{−2})G}, and Re u ≤ (1−ρ_0)^{−2}x^{−2} = 1.2346x^{−2} at ρ_0 = 0.1. Per scale-k unit cube (≤ 4 free links): e^{1.07(N²−1)(1+η_x)}. Still O(1) per cube, inside the p. 380 budget O(1)M^dR^{d+1}d′ with a larger O(1). ## 4. Derivation of (H−) from the CMP 102 statements
Units: block units of scale k, L^kη = 1, η = L^{−k} (CMP 122 I convention, (1.80)). B = (1/i)log V′ = MA's X. h = Y − X, ‖h‖ = sup_b‖h_b‖, ‖h‖²_2 = Σ_b‖h_b‖²_2.
4.1 Exact chain rule [P]. A(ζ_i, ·) = Σ_p ζ_pP_p, P_p = Re tr(1 − U(∂p)), is gauge invariant plaquette by plaquette (ζ_i is a plaquette weight, 0 ≤ ζ_i ≤ 1). So the axial vs Landau gauge choice for U_k(V′V_0) does not change F. Let Y_p(B) be the fine plaquette angle of U_k(e^{iB}V_0) (Landau form exp iη𝓗(B), p. 305). Then
  D²F[h,h] = Σ_p ζ_p {D²P_p[DY_ph, DY_ph] + DP_p·D²Y_p[h,h]}.   (4.1)
This is the brief's D²A[DUh,DUh] + DA[D²U(h,h)], written per plaquette. No criticality (170) is used, so ζ_i ≠ 1 near ∂X_i does no harm.
4.2 First term [P, MA §4(a)]. d²/dt² Re tr e^{i(X+tZ)} = −tr Z² + O(‖X‖²‖Z‖²) for Hermitian small X. So D²P_p[δ,δ] ≥ −c_P|Y_p|²‖δ‖²_2. With |Y_p| = O(ε_kη²) (4.3) this term is ≥ −O(ε²)·(positive quadratic form) and is dominated by the second term. Not O(ε)-critical.
4.3 Gradient size [V for the bound, I for the constant]. CMP 122 I (1.80): |U_0(∂p) − 1| < 2ε_kη² + O(1)B_3B_5M^5exp(−δ dist(p,Λ))ε_kη². On the cutoff ball |B_b| < M_0ε_k, the first-derivative bound (190) adds O(1)M_0ε_kη². So |DP_p| ≤ |Y_p| ≤ c_Xε_kη², c_X = 2 + O(1)B_3B_5M^5 + O(1)M_0.
4.4 Second derivative of 𝓗 by Cauchy [P given the cited bounds hold on the complex ball]. Write δ𝓗/δB(B) = K_0 + K_1(B), K_0 = value at B = 0. From (179), (188): δ𝓗/δB = (1 − H·δD/δA′)(𝔄_0 + H_0), and the B-dependence enters only through δD/δA′ and G̃(δ²V/δA′²)(𝒜_0 + H_0B). By (55) and one Cauchy step at radius ε_3/2: |δD/δA′| ≤ 8C_2ε_3. By (187): ‖G̃(δ²V/δA′²)‖ ≤ O(1)B_0C_4B_5ε_1. Hence, on the complex ball |B| < 2C_1ε_1 (Prop. 9 analyticity),
  |K_1(B)(x,y)| ≤ c_Nε_1·e^{−⅛δ_0d(x,y)},  c_N = O(1)(B_0C_4B_5 + C_2),   (4.2)
with the decay from (189)–(190). One Cauchy step in the single variable B(y′) at radius C_1ε_1, for real B with |B| ≤ C_1ε_1, gives |δ²𝓗(x)/δB(y)δB(y′)| ≤ (c_N/C_1)e^{−⅛δ_0d(x,y)}. Mixed partials are symmetric, so the same holds with y′ in place of y; the geometric mean gives the two-sided decay
  |δ²𝓗(x)/δB(y)δB(y′)| ≤ (c_N/C_1)·e^{−(δ_0/16)(d(x,y)+d(x,y′))}.   (4.3)
The 1/ε of the Cauchy radius cancels against the ε in (4.2), because 𝓗 − K_0B is quadratic in B ((55), (176)). A naive Cauchy bound on 𝓗 itself (|𝓗| ≤ B_5ε_1) would give D²𝓗 = O(1/ε) and would NOT yield (H−). [P]
4.5 Assembly [P given 4.3–4.4; unit bookkeeping I]. D²Y_p = η²·(local combination of D²𝓗 and D𝓗⊗D𝓗 from the BCH product of four links). By (190), (4.3): |D²Y_p[h,h]| ≤ c_Yη²Σ_{y,y′}e^{−(δ_0/16)(d(p,y)+d(p,y′))}‖h_y‖‖h_{y′}‖, c_Y = O(1)(c_1² + c_N/C_1), c_1 the (190) constant. Multiply by |DP_p| ≤ c_Xε_kη², sum over the O(η^{−4}) fine plaquettes per block, and apply the Schur test (2ab ≤ a² + b²) with Σ_y e^{−(δ_0/16)d} ≤ K(δ_0, d):
  D²F[h,h] ≥ −c_Hε_k‖h‖²_2,  c_H = O(1)·c_X·(c_1² + c_N/C_1)·K(δ_0, d)² + O(ε_k)·(4.2 term).   (H−)
4.6 Uniformity. Scale k: Balaban's norms |·|_{(−1)}, |·|_{(−2)}, |·|_{(−3)} carry the factors (L^jη); in block units they are scale-free, and Theorem 1 says a_0, a_1, B_3 depend on d, L only; C_1, B_5 are absolute (p. 305). So c_H is k-free. [V for the quotes; I that C_2, C_4, B_0 of [4], [5] are also d, L only] Volume: every bound is a local sup bound with exponential decay; the Schur constant K does not see |Λ_i|. [P] Background: Prop. 9 states analyticity and the bounds for every external U in the regular class (3.35)–(3.38) of [5]; (1.78), (1.80) put V_{Λ_i}, U_0 in that class ("U_0 belongs to … the analyticity domains", CMP 122 I p. 195). [V quote; I that the class matches]
4.7 Conditions not checked [R]. (C1) The cutoff ball must sit inside Prop. 9's ball with room: M_0ε_k ≤ C_1ε_1 (CMP 122 I Prop. 1 gives analyticity only for |B′| < ε). If the room is a fixed fraction, c_H changes by an O(1) factor; if M_0ε_k ≥ 2C_1ε_1, the derivation fails. (C2) CMP 102 treats U_k on the full lattice; (1.100) uses the localized U_{k,X_i}. The decay in y alone ((190), stated) is not enough for the ℓ² form.
