## 2. (M-A′): the A_min bookkeeping
Notation. F(V′) = A(ζ_i, U_{k,X_i}(V′V_{Λ_i})) on the cutoff ball B (product of balls, MA §3). A_min = min_B F = F(X*), a number: it depends on V_{Λ_i} and on the exterior, not on V′. G = F − A_min ≥ 0. D^A(s) = ∫_B δχ e^{−sF} dV′, D^G(s) = ∫_B δχ e^{−sG} dV′. Then D^A(s) = e^{−sA_min}D^G(s) for every complex s. [P, trivial]
2.1 Normalized ratios see only G [P]. For every s with D^A(s) ≠ 0: e^{−sF}/D^A(s) = e^{−sG}/D^G(s). So Balaban's ratio at real u is N_u/D_u = e^{−uG}/D^G(u), and the frozen ratio is N_x/D_x = e^{−x^{−2}G}/D^G(x^{−2}). A_min cancels exactly in each. (1.102) holds for the frozen ratio as an exact identity at any complex u. [P]
2.2 How HHOL_LF §3.4(c) writes it [V, SOURCES_HHOL_LF §3.4(c)]. Quote: "the mismatch between the frozen ratio density (x_k^{−2}) and the action at u on Λ_i is the factor e^{−(u − x_k^{−2})A(ζ_i,·)}. It multiplies a positive integrand … the factor times the real density N_x/D_x integrates to D_x(x^{−2} − |δ|)/D_x(x^{−2})." So HHOL_LF forms N_x/D_x · e^{−(u−x^{−2})F} = e^{−uF}/D^A(x^{−2}). That is the UNNORMALIZED numerator at u over the normalizer at x. Its total mass is D^A(u)/D^A(x^{−2}) = e^{−(u−x^{−2})A_min}·D^G(u)/D^G(x^{−2}). The first factor has modulus up to e^{θx^{−2}A_min}. This is the step that mixes couplings: the A_min inside the numerator is at u, the A_min inside the normalizer is at x. [P, located]
2.3 The fix [P]. The chain after (1.100) extracts e^{−uF} into the new effective action A_k at u (p. 202 quote, §1). Write the frozen ratio as
  N_x/D_x = e^{−uF} · [e^{uA_min}/D^G(x^{−2})] · e^{(u−x^{−2})G}.   (2.1)
This is an algebraic identity (expand F = A_min + G). The three factors are:
 (a) e^{−uF}: the action at u on Λ_i, extracted into A_k. Same as Balaban's chain at coupling u.
 (b) e^{uA_min}/D^G(x^{−2}): V′-independent. In Balaban's ratio at real u the same slot holds 1/D_u = e^{uA_min}/D^G(u). So e^{uA_min} sits at the SAME coupling u as in Balaban's chain. It is entire and zero-free in u. It is not a mismatch. Only the G-normalizer is frozen: 1/D^G(x^{−2}) is real, positive, and g-free (a frozen normalization constant, the same convention as (M-D)).
 (c) e^{(u−x^{−2})G}: the true mismatch. It contains G, not F.
So the coupling-mixing factor e^{±(u−x^{−2})A_min} never appears if A_min is kept at u in both (a) and (b). The A_min in (a) and in (b) cancel inside (2.1), as they must, since the left side is A_min-free. [P]
2.4 Is A_min a constant on the ball? Yes. A_min = F(X*) is the value at the minimizer X* of F over B̄ (MA §3). It is a function of (V_{Λ_i}, exterior), not of V′. Note: X* is the minimizer of the (1.100) exponent with ζ_i on X_i. It need not equal V′ = 1, because Balaban's Prop. 1 minimizer V_Λ minimizes A(U_{k,Z}(·)) without ζ_i on Z (MA §2). The cancellation in 2.1 does not use where X* sits. [P]
