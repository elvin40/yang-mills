## 7. CMP 116 p. 3 (image) — the s-rule, and item (a) (Enl)
- Printed: walk (1.6) Σ_ω R_0(X_0)R_{α_1}(X_1)···R_{α_n}(X_n), X_i "unions of connected families containing several cubes from π_k(1), i.e. of the size M_1 instead of M". "Each factor ... depends on external gauge field configurations restricted to the corresponding domain X̃_j^5, and its kernel vanishes in a ... neighborhood of ∂X_j, e.g. ... width ⅓M_1." σ_0 = cubes of size M (R_1M_1M^{−1} ≤ 1). "for a random walk ω localized in X̃_0^5 ∪ X̃_1^5 ∪ … ∪ X̃_n^5 we take ... all cubes from σ_0 which intersect this localization domain, and we multiply ... by s(Δ_1)···s(Δ_m)." Applied to H(s), G̃(s), H_0(s).
- CMP 116 is the [I] setting (whole torus, no Λ). So X̃_i^5 there is a whole-lattice set. No Λ-version of the s-rule is printed in CMP 116, CMP 119 p. 276, or CMP 99 Sect. C (§6). Answer to (a): Balaban prints NEITHER X̃^5 nor X̃^5 ∩ Λ for the Λ-walks. (Enl) is therefore a reading/convention [I], not [V] as the author tags it.
- Is (Enl) admissible as a convention? Yes [P]. The s-dependence is an interpolation; any rule that gives the original operator at s = 1 and keeps the bounds is valid. Taking σ_0 on the whole lattice and the whole-lattice sets X̃_i^5 for the s-product gives: walks whose X̃^5 leaves Λ carry an s of a cube outside Λ; they go into terms whose Y_0 meets Λ^c, which are boundary terms (p. 276 allows such domains). So under (Enl) Theorem BDRY needs no margin.
- Alternative convention (s-cubes only in Λ, effective domain X̃^5 ∩ Λ): then a walk with X_i ⊂ Y_0 next to ∂Λ survives and its Dirichlet-on-Λ factor differs from the whole-lattice one. A margin is then needed: d(Y_0, Λ^c) ≥ width of the ~5 enlargement. Size: the enlargement is ~5–6 M_1-blocks (CMP 116 walks use M_1, not M), and M_1 ≤ M/R_1. So the margin is ≤ one M-cube layer when R_1 ≥ 6 (R_1 a power of L ≥ 2; holds for L = 13 with R_1 ≥ L). Not 5 M-cube layers. The CMP 99 count (□ of side 2M, □^5 of side 12M) applies only if the walks are taken at size M, which CMP 116 says they are not.
- Verdict (a): no margin is printed at step k+1; (Enl) is not printed either. Theorem BDRY holds as stated under the (Enl) convention, which is admissible; under the other convention it holds with a margin of ≤ one M-cube layer, also admissible because those terms go to B. Either way p. 278's "do not depend on Λ_{k+1}" is not literally true of an unspecified construction; it is true of a construction the project may fix. Status: CORRECTED (tag (Enl) as [convention, P-admissible], not [V]).
