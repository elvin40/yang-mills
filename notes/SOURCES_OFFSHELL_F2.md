## 0. Result

(a) F has a walk expansion of the CMP 116 (1.6)–(1.7) type. F = Σ_n(G̃_0T_1)^nH_0 is a Neumann series of the same shape as Balaban's G_1 series CMP 99 (3.138) [V p. 423], which he expands by "replacing the operators … by their random walk expansions". I write the walk terms of F as ordered products of walk blocks of G̃_0, H_0 and the chain DRG′ = DG′ − DG′Q′*C′Q′G′G′ of WG_FULL §2.3, with the local factor Γ(·, J) merged into a block. The concatenation lemma (A1_WALK_FULL 5.4) gives the term bound. Each T_1 insertion adds at most five junctions and carries the factor c_J, so the walk step factor stays ≤ c_0/M_1 if K_TL³α_0 ≤ (c_0/M_1)^5. Derivatives (∇, Δ_U, the Hölder seminorm) act only on the first block, which is a G-walk block of Balaban's own expansions. So every walk term of F, and the s-dependent F(s) of the CMP 116 s-rule, obey the sup, ∇, P_1, Δ and Hölder bounds that Balaban uses for H(s). CMP 116 (1.11)–(1.22) then hold with F(s) in the outer slot, and the localized functions satisfy CMP 109 (3.14), (3.9). Constants: (d, L) and the walk constants; one threshold M_1 ≥ M_F(d, L) and one s-rule threshold on M after κ_1; the cap is implied by the existing WG-1 cap for M_1 ≥ M_F.
(b) The Hölder norm ‖·‖_{1,β} of F does not follow from the ∇ bound at the lattice scale with the right scaling: the lattice route gives η^{−β}|∇A|, which is worse than the needed (L^jη)^{−β}|∇A| by the factor L^{jβ}. The correct route uses CMP 99 (3.43) for G_0, which bounds the Hölder norm of ∇G_0λ by the sup norm of λ alone [V p. 398–399], and (3.133) for H_0 [V p. 422]. Since T_1F is a function with a sup bound, F = H_0 + G̃_0T_1F gets the (3.133) Hölder form with the factor (L^jη)^{−β}. This is the H form, so (3.31) holds for the F-built function whenever Balaban's "Obviously" (p. 276) holds for H.

## 4. Item (b): the Hölder norm ‖·‖_{1,β}

4.1 The norm [V CMP 99 p. 397]. (3.40): ‖A‖_{1,α} = ‖∇A‖_α = max_{μ,ν}sup_{x,x′: |x−x′|≤1}|x′ − x|^{−α}|R(U(Γ_{x,x′}))(D_μA_ν)(x′) − (D_μA_ν)(x)|, "It is understood that the η-scale is used"; a superscript ξ means "functions and distances are on the ξ-lattice". 4.2 The lattice route and its scaling [P]. On the η-lattice, |x − x′| ≥ η for x ≠ x′. So
 ‖A‖_{1,β} ≤ 2η^{−β}|∇A| (|R(U(Γ))| = 1 for real U; 1 + O(Mα_0) for complex U′U, absorbed in the constant).
