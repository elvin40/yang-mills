## 3. CMP 122 II pp. 378–380 (PDF 24–26), page images [V]
- p. 378: "The restrictions introduced by χ_k imply that |∂V_k − 1| < 2ε_k on the corresponding components of Z, and the bound (1.78) [IV] for the function V_Λ. These bounds and the axial gauge conditions for V′_k = V_k(V_Λ)^{−1} imply the bound (1.83) [IV] for V′_k, i.e., |V′_k − 1| < O(1)B_5M^6ε_k … we choose [M_0] as equal to the above bound, i.e., M_0 = O(1)B_5M^6. This implies that the characteristic function χ is also equal to 1." 
  Reading: on small-field components, |∂V_k − 1| < 2ε_k (plaquettes of V_k) and |V′ − 1| ≤ M_0ε_k (links). Together with I (1.98) (§2) the plaquettes of U_{k,X_i}(V′V_{Λ_i}) obey |U_p − 1| ≤ O(1)ε_k with an M-dependent O(1). So P_p ≤ O(1)ε_k². N2 is a correct inference. Caveat: the plaquette bound comes from χ_k (|∂V_k − 1| < 2ε_k) and I (1.98), not from χ(Λ_i) alone; the link bound alone does not give O(ε_k) plaquettes for long axial-gauge paths. In the denominator ∫dV′, χ(Λ_i) is the only cutoff on V′, so plaquettes of U_{k,X_i}(V′V_{Λ_i}) inside the integral obey |U_p − 1| ≤ 4M_0ε_k (four links) plus the U_{k,X} map constant. Still O(ε_k). CONFIRMED with this reading.
- p. 379: "Thus the operation T′_k(X) can be represented as the operation for a regular G-valued configuration U, with the additional small, and possibly complex valued, term in the exponential. All the expressions in the definition (1.71), for the configuration U, are real, and the exponential density in the integral is positive." CONFIRMED. The complex background (U,J) enters only through σ (first order in A′, J). The Wilson term in (1.71) is A((g″_k(·))^{−2}ζ_1Γ_X, U″_{k+2}(exp ig_kB, V″)), evaluated on a G-valued configuration of the real integration variables B. So P_p ≥ 0 inside T′ for every integrand point. O3 Step 1 uses the right quantity.
- p. 380: (1.73), (1.74), (1.75) as quoted by the author. CONFIRMED. "T′_k(X,(U,0))1 is obviously positive, although it may be very small, hence T′_k(X,(U,J))1 ≠ 0".
