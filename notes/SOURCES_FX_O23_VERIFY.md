- Positivity route for T′_{f+ζ}(Y_i)1 ≠ 0 (the brief's question): REFUTED as a proof method. O3 Step 1 bounds the MODULUS of the integrand per plaquette. Non-vanishing of ∫e^{σ_ζ}dμ needs control of the PHASE Im σ_ζ = −Σ_p Im(δw)ζ_1P_p. Pointwise |Im σ_ζ| is up to (3/2)τΣ_{p⊂Y_i}P_p, which grows with |Y_i| (large fields, P_p up to 2N; even at typical small fields ΣP_p ~ g²·#plaq). Lemma D3.1 then gives radius ∝ 1/|Y_i|. The per-plaquette real-part bound does not prevent zeros: for a strongly correlated positive measure, e.g. all P_p equal to one variable x ∈ {0, 2N} with probability ½ each, ⟨e^{−tΣP_p}⟩ = ½(1 + e^{−2Nnt}) vanishes at t = iπ/(2Nn), while every per-plaquette real part is fine. For a product measure the radius would be per-plaquette. So the radius is set by decorrelation inside Y_i, which needs a cluster expansion of the large-field measure (author's repair (c)). Author's "OPEN" is correct.
- Fallback check: |⟨e^{−tX_Δ}⟩ − 1| ≤ e^{|t|B_Δ} − 1 for |X_Δ| ≤ B_Δ and a positive normalized measure: correct. Two gaps. (ii) T′_k(Y_i) contains the fine-history operations T_h(Z_h ∩ Y_i), h < k, with sourced Wilson weights on L^{−(k−h)}-plaquettes.
