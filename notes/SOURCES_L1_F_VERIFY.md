V7. C4. CORRECTED. C4 says: "the whole lattice has no R- or B-terms, so R- and B-inputs of earlier steps do not reach later E-class terms". The CMP 119 integral does not support this. p. 271 [V]: "the expression determined by the sum R′_k = Σ_{j=1}^{k_1} R^{(j)} … has the same covariance property as the expression determined by E_k, and we include it into E_k. The sum of the two expressions, R′_k and E_k, is denoted also by E_k." Here k_1 is the largest j with L^{−(k−k_1)}MR_j ≤ L, so R′_k contains all R-terms except those of the last O(log_L(MR)) steps. This E_k is in the action of (3.29), and hence in (3.37), which defines E_0^{(k+1)}. So the step-(k+1) integrand on Λ_{k+1} does contain R-term inputs. These R-terms are outputs of R-operations, so they depend on the R-operation cutoffs and on the R′-ratio.
 This conflicts with a literal reading of the p. 278 sentence: on the whole lattice (Ω_j = Λ_j = T) there are no large-field components, so R^{(j)} = 0. Either the R′_k contributions to terms with X ⊂ Λ_{k+1} are absent for a reason that is not printed, or the p. 278 sentence is imprecise. I cannot settle which from pp. 259–279.
 What survives. A consumer that uses only whole-lattice functionals is not affected. β_j is one (V6). The frozen map B_j is one too: R-036 builds it factor by factor in the CMP 109 framework (CMP 109 (0.14)–(0.19)), with no large-field regions. For these two, C4's conclusion holds because R ≡ 0 on the whole lattice. It does not hold because "the CMP 119 E-class terms on Λ_{k+1} carry no R-input". Any consumer that needs the identity "CMP 119 terms on Λ_{k+1} = whole-lattice terms" (for example, to transfer bounds from the actual CMP 119 chain to B_j) must cite the p. 278 sentence and note the p. 271 tension.
