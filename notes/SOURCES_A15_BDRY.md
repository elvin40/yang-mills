3.4 Item (3): the whole-lattice β subtraction in finite volume, and the irrelevance threshold [P relative to cited bounds].

 (Thr) Irrelevance threshold: □̃² is liftable (lies in a box of side < N_k/2) iff 5M < N_k/2, i.e.
   N_k > 10M, equivalently N_j > 10M·L^{k−j}.
