## C. The four explicit checks

### C.1 Check (1): the lower bound on |E e^{Δ^c}| at complex t, the smallness condition, and the direction of U1

What FABLE_HARD does (§3.1 (ii), l. 118; §3.2 proof, l. 125). It bounds |Δ^c_ℓ(t, V)| ≤ C_𝒫 := 2C_2Σ_i|t_i|N(f_i)(1 + |supp f_i|_1)/r uniformly in V and ℓ, concludes |E_{ν_ℓ}e^{Δ^c}| ∈ [e^{−C_𝒫}, e^{C_𝒫}], and uses the lower end to make G_ℓ := log E_{ν_ℓ}e^{Δ^c} a uniformly bounded holomorphic family on 𝒫, to which it applies Vitali.

Is the lower bound proved? No. For complex t the integrand e^{Δ^c} is complex and a mean of complex numbers of modulus ≥ e^{−C} can vanish. What is true [P here]: if |X| ≤ C a.s., then |E e^X| ≥ Re E e^X = E[e^{Re X}cos(Im X)] ≥ e^{−C}cos C, which is positive iff C < π/2.
