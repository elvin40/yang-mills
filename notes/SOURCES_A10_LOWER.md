## 1. The field expansion of E^{(j)}, CMP 109 (3.33)–(3.35), (3.54), Lemma 4 [V, page images pp. 277, 280]
(3.34) on image: E^{(j)}(X, U_j(□_0, exp iB)) = Σ_{n=0}^{4} (1/n!)⟨δ^nE^{(j)}(X, U_j(□_0, 1))/δB^n, ⊗^n B⟩ + ∫_0^1 dτ ((1−τ)^4/4!)⟨δ^5E^{(j)}(X, U_j(□_0, exp iτB))/δB^5, ⊗^5 B⟩.
Here B = Q(ηA) is the fluctuation field pulled back to scale j, A = (tζ_□ + t_□ζ_□)H_k(B′) (p. 276–277), and (3.32) gives |B| < O(1)L^jη on □̃^4 ("By the definition of B and by the inequalities (3.32) we have |B| < O(1)L^jη").
Lemma 4 (p. 280) [V]: for U in U^c_{k+1}(□_0, (1+2β)α_0, (1+2β)α_1), A with (3.31), and |B′| < α_3, the configuration U_j(□_0, exp i(τB + B′)) lies in U^c_j(X, α_0, α_1), the domain on which |E^{(j)}| ≤ E_0e^{−κd_j(X)} (CMP 109 (1.18)). So E^{(j)} composed with this map is holomorphic in the extra field B′ on the ball |B′| < α_3.
(3.54) on image: |∫dτ …⊗^5B| ≤ sup_{τ∈[0,1]} |∮_{|σ|=r} dσ σ^{−6} E^{(j)}(X, U_j(□_0, exp i(τB + σB)))| ≤ E_0α_3^{−5}|B|^5 exp(−κd_j(X)). Cauchy radius r = α_3/|B| in σ (so |σB| = α_3 on the circle, inside Lemma 4). Then |B| < α_1L^jη gives E_0(α_1/α_3)^5(L^jη)^5e^{−κd_j(X)}.
Caveat on image (p. 280): the bound holds "on □̃^4 only"; tails with a factor localized in (□̃^4)^c are bounded "by an arbitrary power of L^jη using the exponential decay properties" ("we do not repeat them"). [V; not rerun here]

## 3. Task (2): why the order-n Taylor term cannot be bounded "as in (1.24)" directly [P, arithmetic on printed bounds]
Run (a)–(c) on the raw order-n term T_n := (1/n!)⟨δ^nE^{(j)}(X, U_j(□_0,1)), ⊗^nB⟩, n ≤ 4.
 (a) field bound: |B| < α_1L^jη on □̃^4 (CMP 109 (3.32), p. 280). Unchanged.
 (b) Cauchy in σ on the Lemma 4 disc |σB| ≤ α_3: |T_n| ≤ E_0α_3^{−n}|B|^ne^{−κd_j(X)} ≤ E_0(α_1/α_3)^n(L^jη)^ne^{−κd_j(X)}. Then ∂_{t_□} by Cauchy on (1.22) multiplies by 1/|t_□| = 8B_0C_1e^{16κ_1}α_2^{−1}g_k|B|.
 (c) □′-count: Balaban's (L^jη)^5 → "(6L)^4L^jη" means #{□′ ∈ π_j, □′ ⊂ □̃²} ≤ (6L)^4(L^jη)^{−4} [V, p. 8]. So power p leaves (6L)^4(L^jη)^{p−4}, and Σ_{j≤k}(L^jη)^{p−4} = Σ_{m=0}^{k}L^{−m(p−4)}.
 For p = n ≤ 4 this sum is k+1 (n = 4) or grows like L^{(4−n)k} (n < 4). It is not bounded uniformly in k.
Conclusion [P]. The (1.24) recipe with the order-n power (L^jη)^n does NOT give (1.29) for n ≤ 4. The answer to the task's request "show the resulting bound is the (1.24) form with the order-n power" is NO: the order-n power is too weak by (L^jη)^{4−n+} after the cube count. This is why Balaban's p. 8 sentence refers to "expressions obtained by the transformations described in Sect. I.4", not to T_n. A10 holds only for the TRANSFORMED terms. The transformations change (a) and (b); (c) is unchanged.

## 4. Task (2) continued: the bound for each transformed lower-order term [P given the CMP 109 Sect. 4–5 bounds; [I] where marked]
4.1 The t_□ step for linear-in-δB terms [P]. Every term produced by CMP 109 (4.20)–(4.34) is linear in δB = ∂_{t_□}B|_{t_□=0}, with coefficients depending on B(0) and E^{(j)} only [V: (4.20), (4.24)–(4.31), (4.34): each displayed term has exactly one δB]. Write such a term Λ(δB). The function t_□ ↦ Λ(B(t_□) − B(0)) is holomorphic on the disc (1.22), vanishes at 0, and has derivative Λ(δB) at 0. On the circle (1.22), A = (tζ̃_□ + t_□ζ_□)H_k obeys (I.3.31) with α_2 (CMP 116 p. 7: this is how (1.22) is chosen). So B(t_□) − B(0) is localized in □ (CMP 109 p. 282: "δB is localized in □"; same for the difference, by the same localization of ζ_□H_k) and is bounded by twice the bounds (3.32), (4.17), (4.18) that CMP 109 uses for δB ("The inequalities (4.17), (4.18) hold for the field δB also", p. 285). Cauchy: |Λ(δB)| ≤ 2|t_□|^{−1}·sup_{circle}[CMP 109 bound of Λ with δB counted like B]. The factor 2 comes from writing B(t)−B(0); Balaban's own (1.23) route for the remainder has no 2. The s(Y_0)- and σ(Δ)-circles of (1.10) act on H_k(s(Y_0),B′) only; (1.21) is uniform on |s| ≤ e^{κ_1}; so they give the same factor exp(−(κ_1−1)M^{−4}|Y_0∖□̃^4|) as in (1.24).
