## 1. Priority (1): C0 bookkeeping for the vector Ĵ (Lemma B, 5.5)
What C0 is (0.2). A convention, not a lemma: every block is measured by its localized block norm Δ(y)TΔ(y′), the scale powers (L^jη)^± are absorbed into the weight m = (μμ′)^{−1/2} and an S-rescaling, and then (B1) holds in the uniform form B ρ^{|ω|} m e^{−δd}. WG_FULL writes "we do not repeat it [I, convention C0]".
What 5.5 claims. With Ĵ "in rescaled units", |Ĵ| ≤ c_JMα_0, the double sum over (u, b′) is two Schur sums, each ≤ Σ(θδ_1). Ĵ is never defined. The S-power of each factor (Ŵ, T̂, Ĵ) is not stated. So the step is ASSERTED.
Reconstruction (mine). Scale powers: G, G′ carry +2; (QGQ*)^{−1} carries −2 ((3.132)); J carries −3 ((3.36), p. 419). H* = (QGQ*)^{−1}QG has net power 0. Write each operator as S^{a}X̂S^{b}. In the product Ŵ·(QG)^ the interior S-powers cancel; at the two ends a leftover s(b)^{−1/2}·s(b′)^{−1} (or any split) remains, plus Ĵ := s^{3/2}J. The leftover at b′ is moved to b with Balaban's p. 398 rule: (s(b′)/s(b))^{p} ≤ L^{2|p|}e^{2|p|θ_S d(b,b′)}, θ_S = log L/(RM). So Lemma B holds with constant ×L^{O(1)} and with the rate δ_1 lowered by O(θ_S) inside the branch. Result |w(b)| ≤ C·Mα_0·(L^jη)^{−3}, which is Balaban's printed (3.136) bound. The arithmetic is exactly p. 422's derivation of (3.136) from (3.36) and (3.133), done per walk term.
Missing conditions. (a) The Schur constants Σ(θδ_1) and Lemma 2.1 at rate θδ_1 = θ·½ε need (2.59), i.e. RM ≥ C(d)log c_0/(θε), and θδ_1 > O(θ_S). Both are "M large after ε". This order is admissible (A1_WALK_VERIFY §6: ε is fixed at a minimal M, Σ monotone), but WG_FULL does not state it. (b) The norm must be the L² block norm (3.46), not the pointwise kernel norm (see log). For the vector, w(b) is a value on the block lattice 𝔅, so its block norm is |w(b)|μ(b)^{1/2}; this is again a conversion of the same kind.
