## 1. S-72 (R-023 item (1))

Question. On the CMP 102 and [15] Sect. F pages: is the averaging constraint on Λ_0 = □_0 imposed exactly, with no residual gauge freedom on ∂□_0? Is Ū^0 = U printed (CMP 109 p. 262 gives M^p = Ū^p)?

Answer.
(a) Exact constraint: yes [V]. CMP 102 imposes Ū^j = V on Λ_j for every j = 0, 1, …, k, including j = 0. The constraint is an equality, not a bound.
(b) No residual gauge freedom on Λ_0, which contains every point of □_0 next to ∂□_0: yes [V]. The invariance group of the constrained space is u(y) = 1 for y ∈ 𝔅_k ⊇ Λ_0. The later Landau-gauge step uses only transformations with u = 1 on Λ_0, and Balaban writes this out.
(c) Ū^0 = U is not printed as an equation on any page read [V for absence on these pages]. Five printed j = 0 uses force it [P]. Grade of "Ū^0 = U": [P], not [V].
(d) Consequence for R-023 [P given (c)]: the glued field Ũ (U_{k+1}(□_0, M′(U)) on □_0, U outside) equals U on every bond of Λ_0 and on every bond outside □_0. Every plaquette that meets ∂□_0 has all four bonds in this set. So no fine-scale interface exists at ∂□_0. This gives the conclusion of SOURCES_P253_P275 5.2(a) without the invalid v^{−1}-transform step.

Properties referred to [V, CMP 109 p. 253]: M is analytic on sets of small diameter; (0.5) M({U_j^{−1}}) = M({U_j})^{−1}; (0.6) M({uU_jv}) = uM({U_j})v; (0.7) permutation invariance; (0.8) (1/i)log M({exp iA_j}) = (1/n)ΣA_j + higher order; (0.9) M maps G-sets to G. The p. 253 sentence: "The considerations and results of this, and previous papers, do not depend on any particular averaging operation used; they are valid universally for all averages satisfying the above properties."

Lipschitz modulus [P]. For fixed (d, L) the size n = |G(y, x)| is bounded by a number n_max(d, L), since every Γ ∈ G(y, x) is a monotone path inside one L-block. M is analytic on a fixed neighbourhood of the identity n-tuple for each n ≤ n_max. Cauchy estimates on a smaller neighbourhood then give a Lipschitz bound and bounds on the first two derivatives that are uniform over all the finitely many n. The constants depend on (d, L, M), as SOURCES_P253_P275 §2.2 states.

## 3. S-74 (R-023 item (6))

Question. Is CMP 102's M (a multiple of R_1M_1, M ≤ R_1M_1a_1/ε_1) compatible with CMP 109's M under the canon order (N-310)?

Answer. Yes, with one constant correction [P]. Write M_102 for the half-size of a CMP 102 cube (size 2M_102L^jη) and M for CMP 109's M (N-310, M = L^m).
(a) Lower side. CMP 102 needs M_102 ≥ R_1M_1 and M_102 a multiple of R_1M_1 [V p. 300]. The route of SOURCES_A18_REGULAR 5.2(c) takes 2M_102 := c_HLM = 18LM, rounded up to a multiple of 2R_1M_1. Then M_102 ≥ 9LM ≥ R_1M_1, because M ≥ R_1M_1 is an entry of M_req (SOURCES_HIGHER_M1 B3, inside N-310) [V for the canon text]. Rounding up adds at most 2R_1M_1 ≤ 2M to 2M_102. So 18LM ≤ 2M_102 ≤ (18L + 2)M [P]. A CMP 109 cube of size 18LM lies inside a concentric CMP 102 cube of size 2M_102, and the bound of (9)–(10) on the larger cube restricts to the smaller one [P]. The larger cube must still be in the CMP 102 class (□̃ of size (2M_102 + 4R_1M_1)L^jη inside B^j(Λ_j) ∪ B^{j+1}(Λ_{j+1}), p. 279 [V]). That is the cube-class geometry already carried as R-023 item (2) and THM37_FULL 3.2a [I-geo]. It is not new here.
 No rounding is needed if R_1 divides 9L^{m+1−m_1}, for example if R_1 is a power of L. CMP 99 (Regular) p. 77 [V] prints "a power of L" for R, not for R_1. CMP 116 p. 3 [V] prints "R_1 is a power of L satisfying the condition R_1M_1M^{−1} ≤ 1", but there R_1 sizes a new partition σ_k. That this R_1 is the CMP 102 R_1 is [I]. The canon also carries "R_1 (< L [I])" at N-060. A power of L below L is 1, so the two readings agree only if R_1 = 1. So the rounding route is the safe one.

[V for the canon text.] M is fixed at N-310, before N-320 and N-380. So the cap adds no edge into M and no cycle [P].
