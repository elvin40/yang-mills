## 3. Obstacle (i) re-examined: the W4 §6.2 radius is about the wrong variable
3.1 What W4 §6.2 bounds [V, W4 text]. W4 §6.2 models the R-denominator as D(I) = ∫(positive density)e^{Σ_{X∩Y≠∅}I(X)}, with the input ACTIVITIES I in the exponent. The radius (100MR_k)^{−4} is the radius in I.
3.2 Correction from FX_O23 §3 [V there, page image CMP 122 I p. 201]. Balaban's implemented denominator (1.100) is ∫dV′_{Λ_i} δ_{G_i}(V′)χ(Λ_i)exp[−g_k^{−2}A(ζ_i, U_{k,X_i}(V′V_{Λ_i}))]. It contains no E-, R-, or B-terms. So the denominator does not depend on the inputs I at all, and the W4 §6.2 radius in I is void. HHOL §6(b) copied W4 §6.2 without this correction. [P, given FX_O23 §3]
3.3 What remains: the coupling inside the denominator. At complex g_k the denominator is D(u) = ∫ δχ e^{−uA}, u = g_k^{−2}. It is entire in u (compact domain, bounded integrand). It is positive at real u. At complex u it can vanish in principle.
 - Crude lower bound at complex u (Lemma D type): |D(u)| vs the real D(Re u) loses (Re u/|u|)^{n_i/2} ≥ e^{−2ρ_0²n_i}, n_i = number of real modes of V′ on Λ_i ≤ 4d·dim G·(100MR_k)^4. The anharmonic and cutoff corrections are η(x) = O(n_i x p_0(x)³) + n_ie^{−cp_0(x)²}. Non-vanishing by this route needs η < ½e^{−2ρ_0²n_i}. Since n_i ~ (log x^{−2})^{4r} and η ~ x·polylog, this needs ρ_0² ≲ (log x^{−2})^{1−4r}: NOT uniform in k. [P for this route; no proof that D(u) vanishes]
 - A volume-uniform log D(u) would need a cluster expansion of D(u). FX_O23 §4 notes CMP 116 §2 does not apply directly (axial gauge in a box, no decaying covariance at scale k). [open as a route]
3.4 Repair: freeze the R-ratio at the real reference coupling (O2 "Choice A", applied to the coupling). CMP 122 I (1.102) ∫dV_k R′ρ_k = ∫dV_kρ_k holds for ANY normalized density in V′ on Λ_i (FX_O23 §4 [V there]). Choose the density with weight x_k^{−2} (the real reference), and freeze all of Balaban's g-dependent parameters at x: ε_k = x_kp_0(x_k), δ_k, R_k = R(x_k), N, N_0 (as HTHR/HTHR_VERIFY already require for (H-thr)). Then:
 (a) the ratio N_x/D_x is real, positive, and g-FREE. No zeros. [P]
 (b) the g-dependence of the step sits only in the Wilson weight u·A and the Gaussian scale u of the fluctuation measures, on compact domains with g-free cutoffs. Each integral is entire in u. Finite-volume holomorphy on {Re u > 0} by dominated convergence. [P]
 (c) Cost: the mismatch between the frozen ratio density (x_k^{−2}) and the action at u on Λ_i is the factor e^{−(u − x_k^{−2})A(ζ_i,·)}. It multiplies a positive integrand, so only an upper bound is needed. Sup bound: |u − x^{−2}|·ΣP_p ≤ 0.26·O(1)p_0(x)²n_i. That is too big (it is p_0²·(100MR_k)^4, not p_0²·d′). [P given Balaban's lower bound; O(1) not tracked]

Balaban's large-field budget already contains "O(1)log g_j^{−2}|Z_j| ≤ O(1)M^dR_j^{d+1}d′_j(Z_j)" (CMP 122 II p. 380 [V, FX_O23_VERIFY §3]) and beats it by p_0(g) ≳ R(g)^{d+2} ((1.88), FX_VERIFY) and the p. 389 inequality.
