## 1. Where the field-dependent Λ_j enter the project's W factorization

Inputs read: TRUST_LEDGER B20, B11, B13; SOURCES_D2 §5.1 + D2_VERIFY V7; SOURCES_D3 (1.b), (L1)–(L5), item (vii) + D3_VERIFY item 5; SOURCES_D3W8 §1–§2 + VERIFY S7; SOURCES_A23_TSTEP (a).

Three places use Λ_j.
 (E1) W split (D2 §5.1, D3 (vii)). Balaban subtracts E^{(j)}(Λ_j, 1) = Σ_{X ⊂ Λ_j^{(j)}} E^{(j)}(X, 1) (CMP 119 (2.25)). The index set {X ⊂ Λ_j^{(j)}} depends on Λ_j. D3 (vii) already splits Σ_{X⊂Λ_j} w = Σ_{all X} w − Σ_{X⊄Λ_j} w: the first sum goes into W_k[f]; the second goes into e_k^f, a large-field activity. D3_VERIFY item 5 CONFIRMED this split (CMP 119 pp. 262, 279).
 (E2) (W4) (D3W8 §2.2). The input collection I = {I^{(j)}(X)} is indexed by slots (j, X), X ⊂ Λ_j. The output map G(I) is "restricted to X ⊂ Λ_{k+1}". So the slot set of the inputs and the slot set of the outputs depend on Λ_1, …, Λ_{k+1}. (W4) asserts holomorphy of G on the ball ‖I‖ < 2E_0 in this slot space.
 (E3) (P8) (D3W8 §1). The t-measure integrates A restricted to Λ_{k+1} (CMP 119 (1.26), (3.27)), with covariance C^{(k)}(Λ_{k+1}). The sourced covariance C_f^{(k)} = (C_0^{−1} − g_k²𝒲_S)^{−1} uses C_0 = C^{(k)}(Λ_{k+1}).

What each place needs. In every case the needed property is a statement for ONE fixed sequence of regions:
 (N1) the factorization identity holds term by term in the sum over region sequences, with W_k[f] independent of the term;
 (N2) for a fixed region sequence, the slot space of (W4) is a fixed finite set, and the ball radius 2E_0 does not depend on which sequence is fixed;
 (N3) for a fixed Λ_{k+1}, the covariance change and the domination of §1.4 hold with constants independent of Λ_{k+1}.
The issue would be real only if Λ_j were a function of the integration variable INSIDE one term (then holomorphy in I and in f would act on a domain that moves with the field). Section 2 shows this never happens: Λ_j are summation indices, and the field dependence lives in the characteristic functions χ, which multiply the term and are not differentiated.

2.2 Lemma Λ (structure) [P, from F1–F5]. Fix k. (a) For each region sequence σ = ({Ω_j}, {Λ_j}, {S_j})_{j≤k+1}, the term T_{k+1}^σ is a product of characteristic functions χ^σ (the only field-dependent objects that select σ) and an integral whose integrand, domain of integration (A restricted to Λ_{k+1}), covariance C^{(k)}(Λ_{k+1}), and localization index sets {X ∈ D_j : X ⊂ Λ_j} are all fixed by σ. (b) On a finite torus there are finitely many σ (each Ω_j, Λ_j, S_j is a union of cubes of a fixed size from a finite set). (c) χ^σ never enters the activities: E, R, B are defined on the support of χ^σ by integrals at fixed σ. Proof. (a) is (F1)–(F2) at j = 1 and (F5) with p. 278–279 at step k+1: the integral is "the conditional integral with respect to the variables A restricted to Λ_{k+1}" with fixed covariance; the output is booked as E (X ⊂ Λ), R″ (X ⊂ Λ), or B (the rest). (b) is the finite torus. (c): the activities are defined as logarithms of the conditional integrals of (1.26)/(3.37), in which χ^{(0)}(Λ_1) is the small-field cutoff of the integration variable (part of the integrand, inside the cluster expansion), and the large-field χ's localized in Ω ∩ Λ^c multiply the term from outside (p. 253 "the product of all the characteristic functions in (1.25) localized in Ω_1 ∩ Λ_1^c by χ(Ω_1 ∩ Λ_1^c, S_1)"). ∎
Consequence. "Field-dependent Λ_j" means: the field selects which σ-term is non-zero, through χ^σ. Inside one σ-term, Λ_j is a constant set. No derivative in the input activities I, in t, or in f ever acts on χ^σ: holomorphy statements are made at fixed σ, and the χ's multiply afterwards. [P]

3.2 Lemma P (exact partition) [P, from F1, F5–F7]. Every decomposition is 1 = χ + (1 − χ) or 1 = Σ_R Π χ (CMP 119 (1.18)) with sharp characteristic functions. A product of such decompositions is a partition of unity: for every field configuration exactly one region sequence σ has χ^σ = 1, all others give 0. So Σ_σ χ^σ ≡ 1 pointwise, the sum is finite on the torus, and no field configuration is counted twice. Hence any identity proved for each fixed σ on the support of χ^σ holds for the full density. [P; reading "sharp" from the definitions (1.18), (1.22)–(1.28): all are indicator functions of inequalities, [V on pp. 182–183]. ## 4. (W4) per fixed region sequence; the E_0 → 2E_0 ball does not depend on Λ_j

4.1 The χ's never depend on the activities [P from F6–F7 and CMP 119 (1.18), (1.22)–(1.25)]. Every characteristic function in the construction is the indicator of an inequality on fields: |U(∂p) − 1|, |A(b)|, |V_jV_Z^{−1} − 1|, |B′(b)| against thresholds ε_j, δ′_j, δ_j (CMP 122 I (1.22)–(1.28) [V], CMP 119 (1.18), (1.23) [V]). Thresholds are functions of the reference couplings only (D3 §1: "Reference couplings g_j … fix ALL thresholds"; HHOL_LF 4.1: frozen at x*). No χ contains an activity E, R, B, or the source f. Consequence: σ is a function of the field configuration alone. (W4) is a statement about the dependence on I at FIXED field arguments (U, J) and fixed integration structure. So when I varies, σ does not move. Holomorphy in I is therefore always a fixed-σ statement; there is no domain that moves with I. [P]

4.2 (W4) at fixed σ reduces to the σ-free map [P rel. Claim L_G]. At fixed σ, the slot spaces are the finite sets S_in(σ) = {(j, X): X ∈ D_j, X ⊂ Λ_j, j ≤ k} and S_out(σ) = {X ∈ D_{k+1}: X ⊂ Λ_{k+1}}. By Claim L_G, for X ∈ S_out(σ), G^σ(I)(X) = 𝒢_X(I|_X, U|_X, J|_X), with 𝒢_X the same map for every σ with X ⊂ Λ_{k+1} (and the same as the whole-torus map). I|_X uses slots (j, X′), X′ ⊂ X; by Λ_j nesting [I, inherited from A15_BDRY], X ⊂ Λ_{k+1} ⊂ Λ_j, so all these slots lie in S_in(σ), and the restriction I ↦ I|_X is the same coordinate projection for every σ. Hence: (W4) for G^σ on {‖I‖_σ < 2E_0} ⇔ each 𝒢_X is holomorphic on {‖I|_X‖ < 2E_0}. The right side is σ-free. It is exactly what SOURCES_W4 proves for the regular step (CMP 116 Lemma 1 + Sect. 2 on the small-field integral with χ_k inside; proved given (C1)–(C5), E_0ε_1 → ρε_1). CMP 119 p. 252–253 (F1) licenses using the CMP 116 step at fixed σ: the fixed-Λ conditional integral "is equal to the corresponding conditional integral in the case analyzed in [I] … but with a different background field". The background field enters 𝒢_X only through U|_X, J|_X on Balaban's domain U_j(X, α_0, α_1), over which (W4) is uniform. [P rel. SOURCES_W4 (C1)–(C5), Claim L_G inputs, Λ_j nesting [I]]

4.3 The ball. ‖I‖_σ := sup_{(j,X) ∈ S_in(σ)} sup_{(U,J) ∈ U_j(X,α_0,α_1)} |I^{(j)}(X,(U,J))| e^{κd_j(X)}. The weight e^{κd_j(X)}, the domain U_j(X, α_{0,j}, α_{1,j}) (CMP 119 (2.28): thresholds of the reference couplings), and the radius E_0 (CMP 109 (0.25)/(1.18), a fixed constant of the induction) depend on (j, X) only. So ‖I‖_σ is the restriction of one σ-free sup-norm to the coordinates S_in(σ), and the ball {‖I‖_σ < 2E_0} is the coordinate section of one σ-free ball. The F2′ admissibility bound ‖I_p + I_R‖ ≤ E_0 + 4g^{κ_0} (D3W8 §2.3) is per slot, hence σ-uniform. The Schwarz step of F2′ (disc |s′| ≤ S = E_0/b_k, b_k a max over slots) uses only these σ-uniform quantities; it holds termwise in σ, and the outputs carry σ-uniform bounds. The f-holomorphy domain S_{τ(X)}(X) depends on X and on n(X) (D3 (L2)), not on which σ produced the term except through n(X), which is a property of the slot. [P]

4.4 Boundary outputs. Outputs with X ⊄ Λ_{k+1} are B-terms (F4). (W4) is not claimed for them (D3W8 §2.2 restricts G to X ⊂ Λ_{k+1}); they need only Balaban's B-bounds, and their sourced form is D3 (iii). So the σ-dependence of the split "interior vs boundary" is a bookkeeping choice made at fixed σ and causes no holomorphy question.
