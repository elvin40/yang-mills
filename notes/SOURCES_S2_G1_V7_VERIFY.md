## V4. Does the two-constant theorem remove the complex input?
- Set-up check: for real z the configuration (exp(iL^{−1}η(A′_R + zA′_I))U, J_R + zJ_I) is a real regular configuration if |A′_R + zA′_I|, |∇·| stay below the A7 regularity bounds. The point z = i is the complex point. The disc |z| < R ≈ α′/α stays in the α′-space. Two-constant (Nevanlinna) on the slit disc: log|g(i)| ≤ ω log m + (1 − ω) log M_sup, ω = harmonic measure of the real segment seen from i. ω > 0, and ω → 1 as R → ∞ (ω = 1 − O(1/R)). So complex decay at rate ωδ_A, prefactor M_sup^{1−ω}m^ω. The sketch is CORRECT.
- But it needs M_sup: a quantitative sup bound on the α′-space. Analyticity without a bound gives no estimate (a function bounded on a real segment can be arbitrarily large at z = i). So the complex input is WEAKENED (sup instead of decay), not REMOVED.
- Can the sup be had for free? For C(1) = A_Z^{−1}: a sup bound at complex points needs invertibility of A_Z(U(t)) there, i.e. a complex sup bound on A_Z ((An)) plus a Neumann argument. Still complex. For W_σ(U_c) with σ ≠ 1: C(σ) exists only as a walk sum, so even a sup bound needs absolute convergence of the walk sum at complex U, which is (W^c)-level summability on that point. So W_σ cannot avoid a walk-level complex input.
- Useful refinement [P-sketch]: real walk decay (CMP 99 Thm 3.10/3.15 at real U) + a walk-SUM sup bound (no decay) on the α′-space gives complex decay at rate ωδ_0 for C(1) and for W_σ (W_σ's M-factor becomes e^{−ω·0.506δ_0M}). So the complex part of (W^c) can be downgraded to "convergent with a uniform sup on the α′-space". This narrows the [R], it does not close it.
- Verdict on "no route avoids a complex-U input": CONFIRMED. On "(W^c) is already needed by W_σ": CORRECTED (V3): needed on the α-space by W_σ, on the α′-space by Route C; the α′ version is already in the V9 ledger, so no new item.
